## What to explore

- **Inokashira Park:** pond-side walking, the Benten shrine, trees and water reflections. My photo prompt: a wide pond view, a reflection detail, and a short walking sequence. Blossom conditions for your dates are unconfirmed.
- **Nakamichi-dori:** browse side streets and choose a few shop entrances to photograph.
- **Sun Road and Daiyagai:** shopping arcades for casual browsing and a rain alternative.
- **Harmonica Yokocho:** small alleys with bars/eateries, especially an evening option if you have time.

[Official GO TOKYO neighborhood guide](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/destinations/western-tokyo/kichijoji/) · [Musashino tourism neighborhood map](https://www.gotokyo.org/book1/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/2306_musashinokanko_EN_low.pdf).
