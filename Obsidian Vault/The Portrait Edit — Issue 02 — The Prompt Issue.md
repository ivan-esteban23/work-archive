## Content preserved

Every original prompt, working note, title, and study number is preserved. Chapter assignments remain unchanged. Duplicate and near-duplicate studies remain, including accurate repeat cross-references on 18 study pages. Reference images retain their original pixels and dimensions, without cropping, retouching, distortion, or upsampling. The neutral heading is **Image Prompt**.

The 174 study count and 170 unique reference count describe different things: some studies reuse reference files. Repeated studies are intentionally retained.
