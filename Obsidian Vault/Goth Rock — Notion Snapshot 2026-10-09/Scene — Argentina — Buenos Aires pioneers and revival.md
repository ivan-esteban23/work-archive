## Context

Euroshima's *Gala* (1987, Polygram) is described as one of the first gothic records in Latin America; the band played Freedom, Die Schule, Cemento and Obras in 1980s Buenos Aires. After ~22 years inactive the founders reformed in 2023 with Verónica Neumann and released *Desolación* (December 2024). [rock.com.ar](https://rock.com.ar/artistas/euroshima/) · [Revista La Acacia, March 2025](https://lacacia.com.ar/2025/03/23/euroshima-buenos-tiempos-para-el-gotico/) · [Crónica Sonora on the 2022 Chile return](https://cronicasonora.cl/2022/05/16/euroshima/)

## Listen through the scene

Euroshima's own history runs from new-wave roots (Jay Jay, shared bills with Sumo and Los Violadores) through darkwave to a synth-heavier present — "dramático, sigue siendo dramático" in the members' own words. Las Eras works the opposite pole: suburban sequenced minimalism. The two together bracket the scene's range.

## Documented Argentine bands

Verified 10 October 2026 against the cited sources.

- **Euroshima** — Buenos Aires/Cañuelas; formed 1986 (Fabián Iribarne, José Wyszogrod, Ricardo Parrabere, vocalist Wanda/Silvia Rodríguez); *Gala* (1987, Polygram; Dark Entries vinyl reissue; Twilight Records CD 2020); reformed 2023, *Desolación* (December 2024). Played World Goth Day Chile 2022 (Espacio El Ángel, Santiago) and TEF bills. Note: members rejected the gothic label while making a record universally filed as darkwave. [Bandcamp](https://euroshima.bandcamp.com/album/gala) · [Twilight Records](https://twilight-records.com.ar/releaseseuroshima.html) · [MuyPunk interview 2021](https://www.muypunk.com/2021/03/euroshima.html)
- **Las Eras** — Lanús Oeste; formed 2019 (Lucas Loto, Agustín Sperlee, Sofía Castro); coldwave/darkwave/post-punk; *Volumen 1* (2020), *Austral* (EP, 2019), *Incómodo* (single, 2025), *La caída del velo* (disco, **30 July 2026** — in this year's releases note). Played TEF 2026 (Lima). [Trama Sonora](https://tramasonora.com/artistas/las-eras) · [LA LA LISTA profile, 2020](https://www.lalalista.com/2020/05/18/artist-profile-las-eras-suburban-analogue-darkwave/)
- **Art Nouveau, La Sobrecarga, Fricción, El Corte** — 1980s Buenos Aires contemporaries named in the Euroshima histories; discographies unverified. Treat as leads.
