## What this entry establishes

First Drop Dead Festival, August 2003 at CBGB, New York (NY Decay Productions); the DIY deathrock and art-punk counter-festival to the German events. Later editions moved to the Knitting Factory and expanded to four days in 2005. Date disputed: the festival's own archived history says 2002, while Wikipedia and nemesis.to say 2003 — kept as year precision deliberately.
