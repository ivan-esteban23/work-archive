## Context

Brazil's dark scene is documented back to the mid-1980s (Cabine C, São Paulo 1984–87) and runs continuously through today's Deepland Festival circuit. The Mission's seventh Brazil visit played a sold-out Carioca Club, São Paulo, 13 October 2024 alongside Christian Death — evidence of a durable audience, not a revival. [Sonoridade Underground review](https://sonoridadeunderground.com.br/2024/10/16/christian-death-e-the-mission-demonstram-a-forca-do-gotico-em-sao-paulo/)

## Listening anchors

- Plastique Noir — *Iskuros* (4th album, Wave Records); *Dead Pop* presented on vinyl at TEF 2024
- Cabine C — 1984–87 São Paulo pioneers (Ciro Pessoa ex-Titãs)
- Ego Eris — *Memento Mori* (2018, Deepland Records)

## Listen through the scene

Documented Brazilian bands span gothic rock, post-punk, darkwave, coldwave and deathrock; Fortaleza's Plastique Noir is explicitly framed as "genuine Brazilian Gothic music" against second-wave UK references. No single Brazilian sound is claimed.

## Documented Brazilian bands

Verified 10 October 2026 against the cited sources; self-descriptions quoted where from artist pages.

- **Plastique Noir** — Fortaleza/Ceará; ~15 years active; five discos; *Iskuros* (4th album, Wave Records) framed by post-punk.com as second-wave gothic rock in the Brotherhood of Pagans / Children on Stun line. Played TEF 2024 presenting *Dead Pop* on vinyl. [post-punk.com, 2022](https://post-punk.com/brazilian-darkwave-outfit-plastique-noir-return-with-their-video-for-manifesto/)
- **Cabine C** — São Paulo, 1984–87; Ciro Pessoa (ex-Titãs) plus members of Ira! and Mercenárias; influenced by Siouxsie and the Banshees, the Cure and Cocteau Twins; described as one of the first and most famous Brazilian gothic rock bands and a coldwave forerunner — though Pessoa publicly rejected the goth tag. RPM Discos. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabine_C)
- **Ego Eris** — Recife; formed November 2016 (Nocturnal EP); debut *Memento Mori* (August 2018, Deepland Records); Marc Nero, Breno Romero, Astroblack. [Spotify bio](https://open.spotify.com/artist/2c5Np7AouKkSma0izMJ0o4)
- **Das Projekt, Escarlatina Obsessiva, The Knutz, Baratas de Chernobyl, Ariel Maniki and The Black Halos, Under The Rain** — São Paulo circuit bands documented via Deepland Fest lineups (2017–2018). Genre tags per bill, not verified discographies. [Concert Archives 2018](https://www.concertarchives.org/concerts/deepland-festival) · [2017](https://www.concertarchives.org/concerts/christian-death-the-beauty-of-gemina-das-projekt-baratas-de-chernobyl)

## Labels and promoters

- **Deepland Records / Deepland Festival** — São Paulo; festival running 2017–present at Fofinho Rock Club; label released Ego Eris. Listed among gothic festivals on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_gothic_festivals)
- **Wave Records** — released Plastique Noir's *Iskuros*.
- **Dark Dimensions** — promoter of the Christian Death + The Mission São Paulo date (October 2024).
- **RPM Discos** — 1980s label of Cabine C.

## Venues and events (documented, not recommendations)

Fofinho Rock Club (Deepland 2017–2018), Carioca Club (Christian Death + The Mission, 13 October 2024, sold out; The Mission also sold out 2022).
