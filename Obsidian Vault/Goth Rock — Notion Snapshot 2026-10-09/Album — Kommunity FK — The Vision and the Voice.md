## Recording dossier

The official discography lists the 1983 vinyl and later CD/digital editions. [Band discography](https://kommunityfk.wixsite.com/kommunityfk/discography). LA Weekly places the band in the early Los Angeles deathrock context. [Scene account](https://www.laweekly.com/the-la-deathrock-starter-guide/).
Credits verified 2026-10-09: Patrik Mata (vocals, guitar, songwriting, cover collage); Matt Chaikin (drums); CE CE (bass); Margaret Arana (synthesizer). Original label: Independent Project Records, IP008. [Wikipedia — Kommunity FK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kommunity_FK) · [Wikipedia — Independent Project Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_Project_Records).
Recorded live in the studio with minimal overdubs. Patrik Mata, on the approach: "It was more like bam-bam-bam, we're just going to kick it out and what you hear is what you get." [Beatique interview, November 2025](https://www.beatique.net/kommunity-fk-returns-to-l-a-for-independent-project-records-anniversary-shows).
Edition conflict kept visible: Wikipedia dates the Cleopatra CD to 1993; the band's own discography says 1994/1998 (discontinued). One source quotes Mikey Bean's *Phantoms* as "recorded 1981, released 1983" — single-sourced. [post-punk.com, 2025](https://post-punk.com/deathrock-101-mikey-beans-exhaustive-phantoms-the-rise-of-deathrock-from-the-la-punk-scene-book-is-finally-out).
