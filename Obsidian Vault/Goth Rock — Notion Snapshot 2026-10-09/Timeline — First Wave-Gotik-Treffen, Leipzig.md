## What this entry establishes

First official Wave-Gotik-Treffen, held at the Eiskeller club (now Conne Island), with roughly 2,000 attendees and about eight acts. [Wikipedia — Wave-Gotik-Treffen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wave-Gotik-Treffen) · [Kreuzer Leipzig](https://kreuzer-leipzig.de/2022/06/04/wow-da-kommt-sogar-einer-aus-bitterfeld). Earlier Potsdam precursor gatherings (1987–1989) are contested and treated as year-span history, not as this milestone. Checked 2026-10-09.
