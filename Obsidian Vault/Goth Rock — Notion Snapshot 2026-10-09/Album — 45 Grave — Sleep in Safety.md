## Recording dossier

Apple Music identifies the 1983 release and describes horror-punk and harder-rock overlaps; its evaluation is platform editorial opinion. [Catalog metadata](https://music.apple.com/us/album/sleep-in-safety/162551605).
Credits verified 2026-10-09: Dinah Cancer (vocals); Paul B. Cutler (guitar, keyboards, co-production); Rob Graves / Rob Ritter (bass); Don Bolles (drums); Paul Roessler (keyboards). Produced by Craig Leon and Paul B. Cutler; recorded at Capitol Studios, Hollywood. [Wikipedia — Sleep in Safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleep_in_Safety) · [Discogs master](https://www.discogs.com/master/76476-45-Grave-Sleep-In-Safety).
Original label: Enigma Records, LP ENIGMA 13 / ENIGMA 0013. The 1993 Restless CD added three bonus tracks ("Riboflavin", "School's Out", "Partytime" single version) and the 2022 Real Gone Music edition is a colored-vinyl reissue — neither is the original sequence. [Discogs master](https://www.discogs.com/master/76476-45-Grave-Sleep-In-Safety).
Dinah Cancer, drummer Don Bolles, and keyboardist Paul Roessler appear in the scene account. This does not establish a complete track-by-track personnel sheet. [Liz Ohanesian, LA Weekly, 2009](https://www.laweekly.com/the-la-deathrock-starter-guide/).
