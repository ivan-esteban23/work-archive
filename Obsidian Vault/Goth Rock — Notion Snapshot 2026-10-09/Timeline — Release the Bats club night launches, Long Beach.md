## What this entry establishes

The Long Beach club night launched on Halloween 1998 and ran for twenty years. Wikipedia and Souciant date the first deathrock revival to its founding; it is the documented bridge from the 1980s scene into the 2000s. [Wikipedia — Deathrock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/deathrock) · [Souciant — A Brief History of Deathrock, Part III](http://souciant.media/2012/05/a-brief-history-of-deathrock-part-iii/) · [Long Beach Post](https://lbpost.com/hi-lo/music/death-rock-goth-night-release-the-bats-closing/).
