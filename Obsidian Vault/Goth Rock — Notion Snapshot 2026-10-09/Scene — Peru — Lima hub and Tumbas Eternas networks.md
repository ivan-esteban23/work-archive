## Context

Tumbas Eternas Producciones, founded in Lima in 2011 by Khristian Cordero (described on its own page as having "mas de 3 decadas en la escena Oscura Underground"), is the documented center of the Peruvian dark scene: 60+ international bands booked, a label arm (Tumbas Eternas Records, December 2020) pressing CDs and vinyl across Latin America, and an annual festival. Its 15th anniversary falls in 2026. [About page](https://tumbaseternas.com/informacion/)

## Listening anchors

- Último Refugio — *Aquellos Días* (LP, vinyl via Tumbas Eternas Records; announced, check release status)
- Rod Blur — 4 discos, 3 EPs, 8 singles; supported Ville Valo in Lima
- Plastique Noir (Brazil) + Equinoxious (Mexico) at TEF 2024 — the cross-border circuit in one bill

## Listen through the scene

Lima's documented sound spans post-punk, darkwave, goth, deathrock, coldwave and minimal electronics — the promoter's own genre list. No single Peruvian sound is documented; the hub function matters more than a style.

## Documented Peruvian bands

Verified 10 October 2026. Self-descriptions quoted from artist or promoter pages; festival billing from the promoter and Peruvian press.

- **Último Refugio** — Peruvian post-punk/darkwave; formed **1988**, returned 2019 as a duo (Lucho Málaga, voice/bass; Henry Ramos, guitar/sequences/keyboards). New album *Aquellos Días* announced on vinyl via Tumbas Eternas Records. [TEF 2024 billing](https://tumbaseternas.com/producto/tumbaseternasfest2024/)
- **Rod Blur** — Peruvian post-punk/new wave; 4 discos, 3 EPs, 8 singles; opened for Ville Valo in Lima. [TEF 2024 billing](https://tumbaseternas.com/producto/tumbaseternasfest2024/)

## Labels and promoters

- **Tumbas Eternas Producciones / Records** — Lima; promoter since 2011, label since December 2020. Booked 60+ international acts (She Past Away, Belgrado, Das Ich, Pink Turns Blue, Boy Harsher, Twin Tribes, Ductape, Vioflesh, Diavol Strâin, Sweet Spectra among them). [About](https://tumbaseternas.com/informacion/)
- **Tumbas Eternas Fest (TEF)** — annual, Vichama Conciertos, Jr. Carabaya 954, Centro Histórico de Lima. TEF 2024 (17 February 2024) billed Plastique Noir + Equinoxious + Rod Blur + Último Refugio, covered by [El Comercio](https://elcomercio.pe/luces/musica/plastique-noir-y-equinoxious-llegan-a-peru-para-ser-parte-del-tumbas-eternas-fest-celebs-ultimas-noticia/) and [Rock Achorao](https://www.rockachorao.com/tumbas-eternas-fest-trae-de-brasil-a-plastique-noir-y-de-mexico-a-equinoxious-este-17-de-febrero/). TEF 2026 (27 June 2026, C.C. Kantaro, Av. Tacna 225) billed Las Eras (Argentina) + Sexores (Ecuador/Mexico). [Ticketmaster listing](https://www.ticketmaster.pe/event/tumbas-eternas-fest-2026-venta-general)
