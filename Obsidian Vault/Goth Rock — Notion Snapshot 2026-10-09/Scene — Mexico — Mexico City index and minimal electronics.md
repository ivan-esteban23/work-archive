## Context

Escena Oscura Mexicana, run by Alejandro Darkleto, is a running index of Mexican goth, post-punk, darkwave, coldwave and adjacent bands with entries through September 2026 — the closest thing to a national scene census found. Mexico City clubs El Real Under and El Centro de Salud anchor the live side, with Bogotá-style all-nighters noted as a regional pattern. [Index](https://escenaoscuramexicana.com/) · [Remezcla, 2023](https://remezcla.com/lists/music/vampiros-10-bands-biting-into-goth-darkwave-en-espanol/)

## Listen through the scene

The documented Mexican pole leans minimal electronics and cold wave (analog/modular synths, pre-MIDI 80s reference) rather than guitar-led goth rock. Remezcla's 2023 list places Mexico alongside Ecuador (Sexores), Colombia (Bogotá all-nighters) and the Southern Cone as one regional underground.

## Documented Mexican bands
