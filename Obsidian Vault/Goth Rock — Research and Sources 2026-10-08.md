Broad web searching for this shelf produced heavy noise from social media and playlist aggregators. The reliable route was opening Bat-Cave's own Bandcamp discography directly and checking dates on the release pages. The LatAm picture has since changed: Peru, Brazil, Argentina and Mexico now have dedicated Scene notes (10 October 2026), and Las Eras joined the 2026 released list. Remaining LatAm gaps: *Aquellos Días* status, 2026 Brazilian/Mexican full-lengths, Ecuador and Colombia notes.

## Latin America sources (Peru, Brazil, Argentina, Mexico) — 10 October 2026

Opened 10 October 2026 for four new Scene notes. Promoter and press pages state facts; artist self-descriptions are quoted as such.

**Peru**
- **[Tumbas Eternas — about](https://tumbaseternas.com/informacion/)** — Lima promoter since 2011 (Khristian Cordero, "3 decadas en la escena Oscura Underground"); 60+ international bands; label arm since December 2020; 15th anniversary 2026.
- **[TEF 2024 billing](https://tumbaseternas.com/producto/tumbaseternasfest2024/)** — 17 February 2024, Vichama: Plastique Noir (BR) + Equinoxious (MX) + Rod Blur + Último Refugio (PE). Source for both Peruvian bands' existence and discography claims.
- **[El Comercio, 16 February 2024](https://elcomercio.pe/luces/musica/plastique-noir-y-equinoxious-llegan-a-peru-para-ser-parte-del-tumbas-eternas-fest-celebs-ultimas-noticia/)** — Major Peruvian newspaper corroborates the TEF 2024 bill and calls Plastique Noir one of Latin America's most important post-punk/darkwave/gothic bands (attributed opinion).
- **[Rock Achorao, 20 January 2024](https://www.rockachorao.com/tumbas-eternas-fest-trae-de-brasil-a-plastique-noir-y-de-mexico-a-equinoxious-este-17-de-febrero/)** — Independent Peruvian outlet; corroborates bill plus Último Refugio's 1988 formation and 2019 return.

## Evidence gaps

Detailed scene histories, albums beyond the starting three, independent 2026 reviews, international discovery, and annual candidate pools still require research. Release metadata does not establish musical quality; platform tags and promotional copy do not settle classification. Exact-day discrepancies are preserved rather than silently resolved.

Added 10 October 2026: independent review coverage is the largest remaining gap — only *Executing Nostalgia* and *When Death Leaves* have a located third-party review among the newly added titles. Core gothic rock eligibility for Kylmä Krypta and The Last Decade currently rests on artist and label framing rather than independent appraisal. The Bat-Cave page for *Omelas* was unreachable and should be rechecked. Corpus Delicti demonstrates why interview chatter is not release metadata: an October 2026 discussion of new songs preceded an album that had already shipped in November 2025. The four Bat-Cave additions carry no credits on their Bandcamp pages, so personnel and production remain unknown.
