# Health Care — corrected working guide
Prepared 2026-10-12 after comparing Gemini and Perplexity. This is a provisional learning guide, not an individualized treatment plan.

See [the full comparison](GEMINI-PERPLEXITY-COMPARISON.md) and [the Perplexity review](RESEARCH-REVIEW.md). Retain the original PDFs separately; corrected conclusions should not erase their original wording.

## Caffeine
Learn dose, timing, serving size, sensitivity and withdrawal before choosing a change. Safety references are not goals. Use the final [EFSA opinion](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/efsajournal/pub/4102), rather than the consultation draft reproduced by Gemini.

Do not assign a universal “six-hour safe window.” The [2024 dose-and-timing trial](https://academic.oup.com/sleep/article/48/4/zsae230/7815486), absent from both reports' main analyses, compared single doses in 23 healthy young men and found different results for 100 and 400 mg. Its limited sample and single-dose design matter. No result guarantees an individual's sleep response.

Observation: identify actual product, amount or shots, preparation and time. Leave caffeine milligrams uncertain when necessary. Do not assume genetic clearance or begin scheduled microdosing from these reports.

## Sleep
Focus on sleep opportunity, estimated sleep duration, regularity and daytime functioning. CDC's guidance varies by age; it recommends seven or more hours for ages 18–60, seven to nine for ages 61–64, and seven to eight for ages 65 and older. [CDC sleep guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/sleep/about/).

Do not shorten sleep to match an observational mortality minimum or a fixed cycle calculator. Tracker outputs are estimates; personal diaries and device stages do not diagnose a disorder. Persistent sleep difficulties merit appropriate professional assessment.

## Food
Study adequacy, variety, food sources, affordability and preferences before choosing a named diet. Avoid equating an observational carbohydrate percentage with an ideal prescription.

WHO's adult sodium recommendation is less than 2 g/day, equivalent to less than 5 g salt. Its original [sodium guideline](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241504836) is dated 2012. Preserve sodium and salt units. This reference is not a tailored medical nutrition plan.

Use [WHO healthy-diet guidance](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet) for sugar definitions and food-pattern principles. Sugar gram equivalents depend on the illustrative energy intake. Intermittent fasting is one pattern to study; it is not a default next step or a universally superior method.

## Movement
Activity is beneficial, and replacing sedentary time with movement is a useful general direction. WHO emphasizes that some activity is better than none and all activity counts. [WHO physical activity](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/physical-activity).

Choose feasible progression after learning current activity and limitations. Do not treat a cohort study's highly active category as a personal dose required to cancel sitting risk. Precise break schedules remain optional practical examples, not guarantees.

## Habits and stress
Begin with one behavior's cue, purpose and immediate reward. Make a desired action easy and attach it to a workable context. Keep occasional setbacks separate from overall progress.

Habit timelines from one study are not personal deadlines. Compare stress interventions by population, duration and comparator rather than assuming a brief exercise equals a full structured program.

## Proposed first lesson
Question: How do sleep pressure, circadian timing and caffeine differ?

Learning activity:
1. Explain each concept in a few sentences.
2. Describe how a caffeinated drink might change perceived alertness without determining total sleep need.
3. Identify one example where dose, timing or individual sensitivity changes the interpretation.
4. Write one remaining question.

Then use [the optional journal](DAILY-JOURNAL.md) for seven days if helpful. Keep entries brief: sleep times, drinks and timing, energy, movement, meals and stress. Record observations before choosing one manageable change. No personal observations have been entered.

## Research rule
A claim enters the personal plan only after its source, applicability and relevant personal context are understood. Agreement between AI reports is a lead for verification. Keep unsupported precision and unresolved contradictions visible.
