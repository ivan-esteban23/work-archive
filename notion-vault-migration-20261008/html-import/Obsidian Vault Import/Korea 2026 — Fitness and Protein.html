Eating enough protein while traveling

Korean dishes vary: choose a protein portion deliberately instead of assuming every meal is high in protein. Keep rice/noodles, vegetables and enough overall food for walking and training. General sports-nutrition guidance for healthy exercising adults is 1.4–2.0 g protein/kg/day, often distributed across meals of roughly 20–40 g. Your body weight and usual targets are unknown, so this is context rather than a personalized prescription. ISSN position stand, 2017
