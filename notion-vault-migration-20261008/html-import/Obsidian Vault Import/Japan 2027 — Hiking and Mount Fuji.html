Mount Fuji — scenic visit around Kawaguchiko

The official Mount Fuji climbing site says all summit trails are closed outside the climbing season and off-season climbing is extremely dangerous. Late March/early April is outside the normal summer season. This plan visits the Fuji area and viewpoints; it does not include a summit climb. Published 2026 access dates are not confirmed 2027 dates. A fifth-station trip also needs separate road/service checks; it is not required for the proposed day.
