Where K-ETA is required, obtain approval before boarding. The official fee is KRW 10,000 plus payment charges; processing is usually within 72 hours but can take longer. An approval is not a visa or a guarantee of admission. K-ETA official application guide

The electronic arrival declaration is a separate, free process, submitted within three days before arrival using Korean time. Valid K-ETA holders are exempt from that declaration; other exemptions also exist. Use the official navigator for your circumstances. Official e-Arrival Card guide; Arrival-card exemption navigator

Money and tax refunds

Banknotes are KRW 1,000, 5,000, 10,000 and 50,000; there is no KRW 20,000 banknote. Carry some smaller denominations. Foreign-card acceptance and ATM fees depend on your card and the machine: check international-network support and the fee screen. Korean banknotes

At participating shops, eligible purchases of KRW 15,000 or more can qualify for a tourist tax refund. Immediate refunds happen at purchase; airport refunds are a different procedure. The official immediate-refund limits are under KRW 1 million per transaction and up to KRW 5 million in total purchases during the stay. Eligibility, export timing and unused-goods conditions apply. Keep the passport, receipts and goods accessible if departure inspection is needed. Comprehensive tax-refund guide

No exchange rate in the old guide is a current quote. Record the rate, conversion fees and date alongside USD/CLP comparisons. International postage to Chile needs a separate service/price check; domestic convenience-store parcel services do not establish international delivery availability.
