My starting recommendation

Compare three Fukuoka nights: arrival and a relaxed Hakata evening → Ohori Park, tea, and Tenjin/Daimyo browsing → Dazaifu OR Itoshima → departure. Four nights make room for both excursions or a slower city day. With two nights, prioritize the city and one yatai evening rather than assume two full excursion days fit.

Fukuoka sits on Kyushu, west of your Osaka chapter. It earns its place when you want another food-focused city or a gateway to a future Kyushu trip; adding it to 15 days means reducing other bases. It is not scheduled as a casual Kyoto day trip.

Choose a yatai area

Yatai are compact food stalls, a different format from a full indoor izakaya. For you, I would compare Tenjin after shopping and Nakasu for river/night photos; Nagahama is a third option if its location and menus suit your day. Choose one area for dinner rather than schedule a three-district crawl. Official yatai area comparison.

Use the city's yatai guide and English stall map to pick a stall closer to the travel date. The map is an orientation source, not confirmation that every stall will operate that evening. Weather and individual schedules can change the plan.

My evening plan: look at the displayed menu/prices → choose one stall with a suitable seat → order a small meal/drink → ask before filming → make room when finished → take your river photos afterward. Keep an indoor restaurant alternative if stalls are closed or queues are unsuitable. Official yatai dining context and limited-seat etiquette.

Your Osaka izakaya plan remains a separate experience. Fukuoka's first food priority in this chapter is a yatai evening; it does not require buying a guided food tour.

Dazaifu or Itoshima — choose the third-day outing

Dazaifu: Nishitetsu Fukuoka (Tenjin) → Nishitetsu Futsukaichi → Dazaifu Line → Dazaifu Station → shrine approach on foot. Subway Tenjin and Nishitetsu Fukuoka (Tenjin) are distinct stations requiring a walk. Official heritage access guide. Plan shrine/approach, lunch, and umegaemochi; add the Kyushu National Museum only if its opening calendar and your pace suit. Museum visitor guide.

Check Dazaifu Tenmangu's current official notices before visiting. Shrine restoration/access arrangements have changed; old images of a temporary sanctuary do not establish what will be open in 2027. No specific sanctuary layout, bridge access, or museum exhibition is promised here.

Itoshima / Sakurai Futamigaura: choose one coastal area, a meal/café, and your photo walk. Official prefectural Futamigaura guide. Compare Showa Bus West Coast Liner from the Hakata/Tenjin area with other dated local options; check the exact boarding point and final return. Treat those stops as research leads, not 2027 confirmation. Coastal spots are dispersed; a train ride to the Itoshima area does not by itself complete the journey to Futamigaura.
