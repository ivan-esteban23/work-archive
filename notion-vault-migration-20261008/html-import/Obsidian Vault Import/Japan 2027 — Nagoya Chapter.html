My starting recommendation for you

Start by comparing two nights: arrive and eat locally → one full city day with a castle OR garden morning, Osu browsing, and a short Sakae evening → depart toward Kyoto or Tokyo. That gives one full sightseeing day without forcing another flight into the mainland route.

Choose three nights if you want a second full day for Inuyama OR Ghibli Park OR a museum-focused city day. Those are alternatives. Ghibli Park earns the extra time only if the films appeal to you and the right dated ticket is available.

With less time, compare a transfer-day stop between Tokyo and Kyoto: store luggage, have one meal, and choose one area. First confirm storage hours/capacity and the onward train. A brief station meal is feasible with a shorter stop; a castle plus Osu plus Sakae circuit needs substantially more time. These durations are my itinerary recommendations, not operator tour packages.

A city day — choose one morning anchor

Nagoya Castle / Hommaru Palace: my first comparison for architecture. The castle operator currently lists the main keep as closed; the reconstructed Hommaru Palace is a separate visitor attraction. Plan for the areas actually open, rather than a climb inside the keep. Current castle visitor guide · Hommaru Palace.

Check current admission, opening, and palace rules before visiting; the subway station is now Nagoyajo, rather than the old Shiyakusho name found in older guides. City castle listing / access.

Tokugawaen: the alternative for pond, bridge, foliage, and quieter garden photography. Tokugawa Art Museum is a separate museum choice next to the garden; check each ticket/calendar instead of assuming one admission includes both. For a shopping-focused day, choose the garden alone. Garden operator · Museum visitor listing.

Atsuta Jingu: another alternative for a shrine and wooded precinct walk. Choose it in place of the castle/garden morning if shrine culture interests you most. Observe the area's photography signs and give worshippers space. Shrine visitor information · Precinct map.

Then take a seated lunch/break → one Osu shopping block → one Sakae evening block. Leave the other morning anchors for a longer stay.

One extra day — choose your branch

Inuyama: my first comparison if you want castle-town streets, river context, and a slower photography day. Use a suitable Meitetsu service from Meitetsu Nagoya to Inuyama Station, then walk via the castle-town streets toward Inuyama Castle. The castle operator gives about 20 minutes on foot from the station. Confirm the actual train, opening/ticket, and your comfort with the castle visit. Return to the same Nagoya hotel. Castle operator access · JNTO Inuyama Castle guide.

The operator currently requires advance reservations; ticket types give different access, and a warehouse entry time needs attention. Select the date, areas, and official release rules before assigning this day. The current sales page covers 2026 releases and does not confirm your 2027 inventory. Official overseas tickets.

Getting here — Tokyo airports and Centrair

From Tokyo / Kyoto / Osaka: Nagoya is a stop on the Tokaido Shinkansen corridor. Compare Tokyo or Shinagawa → Nagoya, Nagoya → Kyoto, and Shin-Osaka → Nagoya using the actual dated service. Osaka Station and Shin-Osaka are different transfer points. Price the whole selected route, seat, baggage needs, and local hotel transfers. JR Central official planning / train information · City intercity access.

From Haneda (HND): compare Keikyu → Shinagawa, then a separately selected Shinkansen → Nagoya. Allow immigration/baggage, terminal-to-station walking, the station transfer, and a flexible connection after an international flight. Keikyu airport access · JR Central.

From Narita (NRT): compare a suitable Narita Express → Tokyo or Shinagawa, then Shinkansen → Nagoya. Confirm the actual N'EX destination and both reservations; the airport train does not continue to Nagoya. JR East N'EX · JR Central. The existing seasonal plan starts with Tokyo nights, so direct airport-to-Nagoya is an alternative arrival pattern, not an extra compulsory transfer.

From Chubu Centrair (NGO): compare Meitetsu → Meitetsu Nagoya, then your hotel transfer. On μSKY all cars require a first-class μticket in addition to the regular ticket; compare an appropriate ordinary-car service if that suits better. NGO is different from Nagoya/Komaki Airport (NKM). Read the actual airport code on any flight choice. Meitetsu airport operator guide.

From Kawaguchiko: compare the dated bus to Mishima, then a suitable onward Shinkansen route to Nagoya. The operator currently requires a reserved bus seat; confirm the dated release and book it once the route is selected. Check that the chosen train stops at Mishima and whether a connection is needed; the bus and rail leg need a realistic buffer. Fujikyu Mishima bus · JR Central · Japan 2027 — Hiking and Mount Fuji. Do not route every train as if it served every station.

Within Nagoya: subway plus walking is the starting comparison; Meitetsu serves the Inuyama/airport branches, Aonami the railway-museum branch, and Linimo the park branch. Consider a city pass only after counting your eligible rides and checking date/line coverage. A city subway/bus pass does not automatically include those other railways. Official local transport / passes.
