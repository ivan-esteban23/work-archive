Which Okinawa do we mean?

This chapter starts with Okinawa Main Island, flying into Naha Airport (OKA). Naha, Shuri, Chatan, Onna, and Motobu are places on this island. The Kerama Islands, including Zamami, are separate islands reached by boat from Naha.

Ishigaki / Taketomi and Miyako are different regional choices, already covered in Japan 2027 — Best Islands. They are not neighborhoods of Naha or automatic day trips from an Onna hotel. Compare one region first; adding inter-island flights and hotel changes requires a fresh route. Official Okinawa overview · Ishigaki · Miyako Islands.

My starting recommendation for you

Compare four nights based in Naha: arrival and an easy local meal → Shuri / city culture and crafts → one Chatan coastal outing → Zamami day trip OR a Motobu / Churaumi Aquarium day → departure. Four nights provide three full outing days. Swap the middle days when conditions and reservations justify it.

This is my simplest first comparison while your driving preference remains open. A city hotel is not a beachfront resort. If beach time matters more than city browsing, compare five nights split Naha 2 + Onna 3, with transfers, resort location, and transport priced first. Do not use the Naha day cards as if every bus/port were equally convenient from Onna.

With only three nights, keep city culture and one coast/boat/north choice. A longer stay can add slower beach days; it does not need an outing to every named area.

Shuri Castle Park: choose a park / heritage visit and check the actual areas available on your date. The operator announces the restored Seiden main hall interior opening for 23 November 2026, with designated date/time reservations for interior viewing. A paid-area ticket without that reservation excludes the main hall interior. The announced adult individual fee from that date is ¥1,000, with no extra charge for the timed designation. Official opening and ticket guide.

Treat this as an announced change checked in October 2026, not proof that a particular 2027 slot is available. Use current park notices and operator access instructions to coordinate your monorail/bus/walk and ticket. Avoid relying on pre-fire photos or older restoration-tour assumptions for what you will see.

Kokusai Street / Makishi area: a manageable meal/shopping block, with the market as a browsing lead. Compare a clearly priced menu rather than assume a seafood purchase includes preparation or upstairs dining. Tsuboya Yachimun Street: pottery browsing and a café alternative. Naha visitor guide.

Choose one seated break and check the actual menu, opening day, and filming permission. Official café / craft walking feature. A named café is a lead, not a booked seat or guaranteed drink.

Coastal exploring — choose a manageable block

Chatan / American Village: waterfront walk, a café/meal, and a limited browsing block. My main photo goal here is late-afternoon coast → sunset if visible → illuminated street detail, with a confirmed return journey. Depot Island / Sunset Walk and the Miyagi Coast are separate possible blocks; choose by your transport and pace. Official Chatan guide.

Onna: compare a beach-oriented stay or one selected coastal destination. Longer transfer time and resort access matter more than the number of beach pins. A paid guided snorkeling activity is an optional separate purchase after checking operator location, equipment, suitability, and weather. Official Onna overview.

Motobu / Churaumi Aquarium: dedicate an outing day to the aquarium / nearby Ocean Expo Park. The operator describes a substantial journey from Naha Airport, approximately two hours by car or two and a half hours by express-bus options before allowing for your actual connection and traffic. This is an orientation estimate, not a guaranteed hotel-to-door journey. Aquarium access · Park's bus information and operator links.

For the car-free version, compare a through bus/shuttle or a suitable dated tour, confirm the actual stop and walk to the entrance, and save the return. Do not combine a full aquarium visit, Onna beach day, Chatan sunset, and a Kerama trip in one day. Aquarium visitor guide. Kouri or other northern detours would require a separate, workable transport plan; they have not been added automatically.

Kerama option — Zamami day trip from Naha

Route: Naha hotel → Tomari Port → the correct reserved boat to Zamami Port → selected beach/village area using confirmed island transport → boat back → hotel. The high-speed boat and larger ferry use different boarding arrangements; confirm your vessel and pier. Tourism association access guide.

Choose one beach/viewpoint or a guided marine activity after coordinating its start/finish with both sailings. Furuzamami Beach is a sightseeing/swimming lead subject to its actual open zones, supervision, weather, and local transfer. A sea-view photo day does not require entering the water. Official Zamami island guide.

Book/check both directions rather than assume a return seat. Boats can sell out or be canceled by wind/waves, and the operator determines daily service. The tourism association supplies information; boat reservations are managed by the village office, not the association. Reservation guidance · Village office for current sailing status and booking links.

Do not schedule this on your flight day. For an island overnight, replace a Naha hotel night and reassess weather/luggage/return buffers first; this chapter does not include that overnight. Do not add Aka and Tokashiki transfers to the same introductory day. If boat conditions fail, use a land day or the northern option when its own transport is workable.
