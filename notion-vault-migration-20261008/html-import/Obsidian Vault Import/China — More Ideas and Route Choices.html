My strongest additions for your interests

I would first add a Shanghai dumpling-making class, one Suzhou garden followed by Pingjiang Road, and a Wukang–Anfu neighborhood walk. They build on your food, photography, cafés, and browsing interests without adding another hotel base. Jingshan is a useful Beijing photography candidate to compare with the existing palace day.

If tea becomes a main interest, two nights in Hangzhou are my strongest route expansion: one lake block and one Longjing village block, with room for transfers. That recommendation is about pace and your interests; it is not a checked train itinerary. The municipal feature describes West Lake and Longjing village, including tea terraces and tea culture. Excursion or new base?

Suzhou: my first excursion candidate from Shanghai for gardens and canal photography. Choose the Humble Administrator’s Garden or research the Lingering Garden as an alternative; UNESCO lists both within the classical-gardens property. One garden, Pingjiang Road, and a meal are a more useful starting plan than buying a package covering several gardens, temples, hills, and a second water town. Compare the actual rail stations, local transfers, admission reservations, and return connection once dates are set. UNESCO gardens · Suzhou overview.

Zhujiajiao: another water-town option within Shanghai, with riverside buildings and Fangsheng Bridge. Compare it with Suzhou on the scenery you want and the complete travel day. I would select one initially and keep the other optional. Boat rides and attraction tickets require separate inclusion checks; a town visit does not establish that every site or boat is included. Shanghai heritage overview.

Hangzhou: allow an arrival/lake block, a Longjing tea block, and an onward-transfer block if staying two nights. Keep tea purchasing separate from workshop fees and ask for the quantity and full purchase price. A provider offers picking, hand-frying, and tasting, but future picking conditions, dates, and language availability remain to be checked in China — Klook Experience Research 2026-10-08.

Chengdu: a route replacement for food, lanes, and an optional panda-base visit. The municipal Sichuan feature identifies Jinli, Kuanzhai, and the Chengdu panda base. Begin with one food/neighborhood day and research the panda outing separately; admission does not guarantee close animal encounters. Specific tea houses and restaurants still need verification. Sichuan overview.

Chongqing: a route replacement if urban night photography and hotpot matter more than Xi’an history. Hongyadong and Liziba are starting points from the official overview, not a finished walking route. Check actual viewpoints, crowd conditions, hills/stairs, river crossings, and hotel access before choosing a base. Do not assume every camera viewpoint is public or every skyline light operates nightly. Chongqing overview.

Zhangjiajie / Wulingyuan: the strongest landscape alternative in this round. UNESCO describes a landscape of sandstone pillars and ravines. Treat it as a dedicated city/park chapter, with weather flexibility and a checked entrance plan. Any Tianmen Mountain or glass-bridge ticket would require its own location and inclusion research; this Wulingyuan source does not verify those products. UNESCO Wulingyuan.

What deserves another research round

Verify a small list of current vintage shops, vinyl stores, and film-related shops with exact branches; this search did not establish enough store-level evidence to recommend names. Develop a food list around the selected neighborhoods and dietary preferences. After the route and month are chosen, check seasonal suitability, holidays, dated transport, admission, filming rules, and complete hotel totals.
