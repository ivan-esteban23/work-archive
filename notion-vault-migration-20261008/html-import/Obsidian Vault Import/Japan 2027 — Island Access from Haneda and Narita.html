Start at the correct Tokyo airport

Haneda (HND), mainland rail route: follow your terminal's signs to Keikyu, choose the service toward Shinagawa, and change there for your onward train. For Tokyo Station, continue on JR from Shinagawa. Some Keikyu services have different destinations or require a change; check the departure display. Official Tokyo Haneda access guide.

Narita (NRT), mainland rail route: compare Narita Express to Tokyo or Shinagawa, selecting the service that calls at your onward station. For a Tokyo hotel, choose the airport route by the hotel's actual nearest station instead of assuming Shinagawa is best. JR East Narita Express.

Continuing by domestic flight: clear the international-arrival procedures, collect/handle baggage as instructed, reach the correct domestic terminal/check-in area, and clear domestic security. Use the airline's required check-in and connection times, including terminal changes. Haneda international-to-domestic guide · Narita international-to-domestic guide · Narita terminal shuttle.

Haneda and Narita are separate airports. If an onward flight leaves the other airport, price and schedule the ground transfer explicitly. A Tokyo airport code on a search result does not establish a same-airport connection. For separate tickets, check who handles a missed connection, baggage charges, and cancellation terms before choosing the itinerary.

For your existing Tokyo-first trip, my recommendation is airport → Tokyo hotel → island outing later. A long island transfer immediately after the international arrival is an alternative only when the actual landing time and connections make it practical.
