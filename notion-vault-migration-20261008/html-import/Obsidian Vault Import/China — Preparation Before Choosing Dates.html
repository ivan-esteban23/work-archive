Entry and arrival

The Chinese Embassy in Chile’s 4 November 2025 notice covers qualifying tourism by ordinary Chilean passport holders for up to 30 days, through 24:00 on 31 December 2026, Beijing time. Do not extend that policy to 2027 without a newer official notice. Other passports and travel purposes need their own eligibility check. Embassy notice.

  • [ ] Confirm passport nationality/type, expiry, and intended travel year.
  • [ ] Recheck the embassy’s current entry conditions for the actual arrival date.
  • [ ] Check every transit country against the chosen flight and existing authorizations.
  • [ ] Save return/onward itinerary and hotel information offline, including Chinese addresses.

Hotels can handle foreign guests’ residence registration; non-hotel stays require a separate registration process. Confirm the accommodation’s procedure before choosing an apartment. Ministry of Commerce practical guide, residence-registration section. The same guide has older visa policy text: use the newer Chile embassy notice for visa eligibility.

Payments, navigation, and connectivity

Official guidance supports linking international cards to mobile-payment apps, subject to issuer verification and app-specific fees/limits. Set up Alipay and/or WeChat Pay in advance, with a backup card and RMB cash plan; actual Chilean-card operation has not been tested. Government practical guide, mobile-payment section.

Google and several other services are blocked on local internet access, according to the UK government’s general China advice. Prepare navigation and translation that do not rely on Google alone. Compare mobile service terms for your actual phone and verify SMS/identity requirements; this draft does not promise any eSIM or roaming service will provide unrestricted access. Tickets and photography

The Palace Museum normally closes on Mondays, with national-holiday exceptions, and does not sell same-day tickets. Foreign online bookings require passport numbers. Plan around that before assigning your Beijing museum day. Official visitor instructions, checked 8 October 2026.

Use the passport details consistently for railway reservations and carry the original document used to buy the ticket. The official English 12306 FAQ supports foreign-passport purchases. 12306.

The Terracotta Warriors visitor page requires advance reservations and displays potentially broad filming/equipment restrictions. Recheck the Chinese and English instructions before bringing equipment; precise still-camera versus video treatment remains unresolved. Museum instructions.

  • [ ] Verify the selected attraction’s booking window, passport entry, closure days, and cancellation terms.
  • [ ] Ask permission for people/shops; check posted photography and tripod rules.
  • [ ] Plan memory cards, batteries, storage, and backups; check airline battery policies.
