Day 1 — 2026-10-27: Seoul arrival

Arrive at ICN, clear formalities and travel to the confirmed hotel. Leave bags or check in according to property policy. If energy permits, keep the afternoon to Insadong, Ikseon-dong and a short Cheonggyecheon walk. Eat nearby and rest.

Keep flexible: Airport terminal, flight time and hotel remain unconfirmed. Skip extra districts after a delayed arrival. Gyeongui Forest Park belongs to the Hongdae day, not this walk.

Day 2 — 2026-10-28: Palace, Seochon and Hongdae

Start with Gyeongbokgung, then lunch and a Seochon bookshop/café block. BOAN 1942 is a sourced candidate, subject to rechecking. Rest or transfer west for Hongdae/Yeonnam and the Gyeongui Forest Park area. Limit shopping to a few confirmed places.

Keep flexible: The guard-changing ceremony is listed at 10:00 and 14:00; confirm cancellation notices. Do not confuse it with other guard performances. Deoksugung is a separate City Hall visit, moved to the next day.

Day 3 — 2026-10-29: Optional DMZ and central Seoul

If booked, follow the tour operator’s actual meeting time and passport instructions. Keep the afternoon flexible because access and return timing can change. After resting, consider Deoksugung and its surrounding streets, subject to palace hours.

Keep flexible: No tour is booked. Confirm Third Tunnel inclusion and walking demands; do not assume JSA access. If cancelled, use a Seoul museum/café day. No Jeongdong festival is assumed.

Day 4 — 2026-10-30: Seoul to Gyeongju

Take a confirmed morning train, transfer from the high-speed station and leave luggage at the accommodation by arrangement. Walk a compact historic-center route: Hwangnidan-gil, Daereungwon surroundings and Cheomseongdae as time permits. Make Wolji the evening priority.

Keep flexible: Train time and admission details need confirmation. Get to Wolji before your desired evening light and verify last entry. The former 20:30 “dusk” description was wrong. Keep the museum optional rather than forcing every site into the afternoon.

Day 5 — 2026-10-31: Bulguksa and onward to Busan

Visit Bulguksa only if current opening times and the selected afternoon train leave adequate margin. Otherwise enjoy the historic center. Collect luggage, travel to Busan and check in. If time allows, explore Nampo/Gwangbok-dong and Yongdusan Park.

Keep flexible: Seokguram is an optional alternative requiring extra travel time, not a quick obligatory add-on. Songdo’s cable car is a separate excursion. Neither is called “Yongdusan cable car.” Avoid assuming Halloween crowd conditions.

Day 6 — 2026-11-01: Gamcheon and Gwangalli

Visit Gamcheon in the morning, then lunch/rest around Nampo or the accommodation. Allow a substantial cross-city transfer to Millak/Gwangalli in time for the waterfront before sunset. Choose at most one café with a verified address.

Keep flexible: Haedong Yonggungsa/Haeundae would be an alternative eastern Busan day, replacing Gamcheon. A Biennale visit is another replacement, not an extra full block. No ten-minute Gamcheon-to-Millak transfer is assumed.

Day 7 — 2026-11-02: Departure from Busan

Check out and travel to Gimhae Airport according to the actual flight confirmation. Keep shopping or coffee local and optional only after airport timing is established. Retain receipts and allow time for any applicable tax-refund process.

Keep flexible: Flight time and terminal are unknown. Work backward from airline deadlines; do not use the earlier 13:00 departure or 11:30 hotel-leaving placeholder. Confirm baggage, connections and transit documents.
