Costs and procedures worth knowing early

Tax-free shopping after 1 November 2026: NTA's announced refund method has eligible visitors pay tax-inclusive prices first, obtain customs confirmation that the goods are leaving Japan within 90 days of purchase, then receive the refund from the business. Carry enough funds for the gross purchase and check each retailer's refund terms. The 90 days is a customs-confirmation deadline, not a promise that a refund arrives within 90 days. NTA April 2025 reform leaflet · Existing detailed shopping guidance — section: 🧾 Tax-free shopping for the 2027 trip.

International departure tax: the tourism agency states JPY 3,000 per departure from Japan from 1 July 2026, generally collected through the ticket. Transitional treatment and exemptions exist. Check the ticket breakdown and count it once; the guide's international airfare sits outside the in-Japan envelope. This is separate from a shopping-tax refund.
