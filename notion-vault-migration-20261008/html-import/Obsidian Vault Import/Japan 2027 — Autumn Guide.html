Walking, clothing, and photo rhythm

Existing trail research gives Takao, Daimonji, and Kurama–Kibune options. Its spring dates are not autumn reservations. Finish hikes in daylight, check access notices, and adapt to wet/icy ground. Choose a city walk if conditions or your preferred effort make the hike unsuitable.

Pack layers, a warmer outer layer for evenings/lakes, rain protection, and shoes with grip. Plan one main outdoor area and a café break rather than crossing Kyoto repeatedly.
