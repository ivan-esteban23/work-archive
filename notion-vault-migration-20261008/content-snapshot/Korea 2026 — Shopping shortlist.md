## Before buying

- Inspect condition, measurements and any damage; second-hand does not mean guaranteed mint condition.
- For records, inspect the disc and sleeve and ask to listen where possible.
- Check the book's language and whether you actually want it.
- Compare full cost, luggage space/weight and the return policy.
- Verify the shop/branch and hours before crossing the city; see [[Korea 2026 — Cafés and Shops]].

Do not count a possible tax refund until the transaction qualifies. Current rules are in [[Korea 2026 — Practical Info]]. International postage availability and pricing are unverified; a domestic courier estimate is not a Chile shipping quote.
