## Airport arrival

ICN terminal and arrival time must come from the flight confirmation. Allow immigration, baggage and walking time before choosing a train.

AREX Express to Seoul Station is listed at **KRW 13,000 per person one way**, with scheduled running times of **43 minutes from T1 / 51 minutes from T2**. These exclude waiting and airport formalities. Buy an Express ticket; do not assume a normal transit-card tap reserves an Express seat. The all-stop train is a separate option. [Airport rail to Seoul](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/entry/getting-to-seoul-from-incheon-airport/)

Seoul Station connects with subway Lines 1 and 4, AREX and Gyeongui–Jungang services; Line 2 requires a transfer. Choose the hotel route after confirming its address. Seoul's adult subway base fare is **KRW 1,550 by transport card / KRW 1,650 single-journey fare**, with additional distance charges; a refundable single-ticket deposit may be separate. [Seoul subway fares](https://english.seoul.go.kr/policy/transportation/modes-of-transport/subway/)

## Busan departure

For a Seomyeon base, the rail route is subway Line 2 to Sasang, then Busan–Gimhae Light Rail to Airport station. The airport confirms the Line 2 connection at Sasang. [Gimhae Airport rail connections](https://www.airport.co.kr/gimhaeeng/cms/frCon/index.do?CONTENTS_NO=3&MENU_ID=110) Confirm first/last services and a door-to-door estimate shortly before travel. For planning, allow about 45–60 minutes from Seomyeon including the transfer, plus a disruption margin; this is an allowance, not a verified timetable. The Sasang–airport light-rail leg alone should not be assigned the old unverified 50-minute estimate.

Set hotel departure from the **actual flight time**, the airline's check-in/bag-drop deadline and the international/domestic terminal. A reasonable initial planning target for an international departure is arrival about three hours before the flight, subject to airline instructions. The old 13:00 flight and 11:30 hotel departure were placeholders and must not be used.
