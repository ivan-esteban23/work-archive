South Korea: preparing a personal trip before choosing the dates
Research date: 8 October 2026

This guide begins with decisions already made about the traveler, rather than a departure date. The current plan is for one Chilean traveler living in San Fernando and departing through Santiago airport. The working ceiling is US$2,500 excluding international round-trip airfare. The diet is unrestricted, with no reported food allergies and a preference for high-protein meals. Season, month, travel year and length remain open, and nothing is booked. Those personal facts come from the live Obsidian notes and the user's 7 October confirmations. The year in the filenames and the dates in flight searches describe research examples. They do not establish a reservation or a deadline.

The proposed route should make space for the interests that give this trip its purpose: photography and filming, cafés, books and records, browsing clothing and small meaningful purchases. A nine-night starting model divides the stay into four nights in Seoul, two in Gyeongju and three in Busan. This is a planning recommendation, not an externally established ideal itinerary. It lets each city have its own rhythm and gives Gyeongju an evening rather than treating it as a hurried stop between trains. A shorter version can omit Gyeongju; a longer version needs a fresh budget. If the international return departs from Incheon, a final Seoul night adds a tenth night unless it replaces a night elsewhere.

Choosing the season changes how those days work. KTO's seasonal guide describes summer heat and humidity, a rainy-season overlap around late June to late July, and a broad June–October typhoon season. Autumn calls for layers as temperatures change. Spring and autumn are therefore useful first comparisons for a trip centered on walking and photography, but that is a judgment about comfort and interests, not a weather guarantee. Winter needs a different clothing plan and shorter outdoor blocks. Compare cities separately and consult forecasts close to travel. A broad seasonal description cannot justify precise daily temperatures months in advance. [KTO Climate & Seasons: https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=140636]

Autumn photographs also require a distinction between a landscape and a forecast. KTO's 8 September 2026 article, using Weatheri's outlook, places Bukhansan's foliage peak around 4 November. That helps explain the earlier late-October/early-November experiment, but does not prove that every Seoul neighborhood, Gyeongju or Busan will peak together. It says nothing certain about another travel year. Dates should leave room for a worthwhile trip even if the leaves change earlier or later. [2026 foliage forecast: https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=1593230]

In Seoul, grouping interests geographically is more useful than filling every hour. Gyeongbokgung and Seochon can form one block, Mangwon and the western neighborhoods another, with Euljiro browsing placed where it fits. The palace's published Tuesday closure includes a public-holiday exception; its November closing is earlier than October's, and last admission is one hour before closing. That makes an opening-day check essential when the actual calendar changes. BOAN remains a bookshop/café candidate in Seochon, rather than an excuse to cross the city twice. [Gyeongbokgung: https://english.visitseoul.net/PalaceArea/Gyeongbokgung-Palace/ENP000072; BOAN: https://english.visitseoul.net/area/boan-1942/ENPk1cyet]

The music stops need equally specific identities. The city's House of Vinyl listing is for the Mangwon branch at 23 Heeujeong-ro 16-gil, with daily 11:00–22:00 hours and a 90-minute limit when full. It is a listening café; the guide should not silently treat it as a record-store purchase. Clique's own ABOUT page locates the shop in Euljiro and lists afternoon hours Tuesday through Sunday, with Monday absent. Its other page's inconsistent information is a reason to confirm before assigning a timed slot. Retaining an uncertain shop name in a research inbox preserves a lead without pretending it is ready to anchor a journey. [House of Vinyl: https://english.visitseoul.net/area/houseofvinyl-mangwon/ENPcq5rwf; Clique: https://cliquerecords.kr/about]

Gyeongju and Busan benefit from the same restraint. The existing guide protects an evening for Wolji and warns that Gyeongju's high-speed station requires a transfer to the historic center. Those transfers still need actual routing once accommodation is chosen. In Busan, the western Gamcheon/Nampo block and the eastern coast should not be compressed into a succession of short transfers. An event must also fit the calendar: the city's 2026 Biennale announcement ends the exhibition on 1 November. It cannot serve as an indoor alternative during the current 2–5 November hotel-search example. Choose a nearby museum or checked café instead when needed. [Busan Biennale: https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/bsnews01/1752281]

The budget should measure choices rather than make old prices look dependable. Its present allocations add to US$2,200, leaving US$300 contingency. Lodging receives US$1,000; meals US$350; cafés US$70; shopping and gifts share US$80. Rail, local transport, commercial gyms, Chile ground travel, optional DMZ, admissions, data, insurance and paperwork occupy the remaining lines. Earlier US$150 shopping proposals no longer apply. An old hotel example totals US$690 before unresolved taxes, but is only a search snapshot. One adult, one room, exact nights, room category, taxes and cancellation terms must be consistent before comparing properties. Buying additional nights because a test airfare looks cheaper can raise the overall trip cost.

Keeping training in the plan does not require paying a large hotel premium automatically. Shilla Stay Mapo's operator lists dumbbells, strength machines and cardio equipment, with daily 06:00–23:00 access for guests aged 16 and over. It does not establish the maximum weights or a full lifting setup. SpoAny's current readable page confirms its City Hall address and facilities, but this review could not reproduce the earlier fee text. Compare a confirmed visitor pass against the entire room premium, and verify registration requirements. Meals likewise need actual portions; an unrestricted diet offers flexibility without establishing a personal protein prescription. [Shilla Stay: https://www.shillahotels.com/en/shillastay/mapo/facilities/fitnessCenter.do; SpoAny: https://m.spoany.com/branch/branch_detail.html?base_seq=ODU%3D]

Preparation becomes concrete once dates and routing are selected. Chile's official travel advice directs tourists to K-ETA, while KTO's exemption extension applies only to included countries through the end of 2026. Neither supports assuming a Chilean exemption or a 2027 rule. Today the direct Korean entry portals displayed maintenance, so their detailed eligibility, fees and processing guidance require another check. Transit requirements depend on the chosen hubs. For medicines containing controlled substances, MFDS's updated manual provides an online application procedure and multilingual instructions; CDC's destination page supports a clinician's assessment of prevention needs, including itinerary-dependent DMZ-region advice. [Chile guidance: https://www.consulado.gob.cl/chilenas-os-que-viajan-fuera-de-chile; KTO exemption notice: https://spanish.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=251932; MFDS: https://www.mfds.go.kr/eng/brd/m_60/view.do?seq=72497; CDC: https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/destinations/traveler/none/south-korea]

The next useful comparison is therefore two or three equal-duration scenarios: usable leave, actual Korean nights, protected flight connections, one-adult rooms and a complete ground budget. The guide can keep its creative ambitions while allowing prices, opening days and entry rules to shape a workable calendar.
