## Before departure

- [ ] Review routine vaccines and itinerary-specific advice with a travel-health clinician; take the actual route, including the optional DMZ visit.
- [ ] Check travel insurance coverage, assistance contact and relevant medical exclusions.
- [ ] Carry medication in original packaging with prescriptions or a clinician's letter where appropriate.
- [ ] Check every medication's active ingredient against Korean import requirements.

CDC advice is individualized by exposure and destination within Korea; do not read this short city itinerary as automatically requiring or excluding malaria prevention or other travel vaccines. [South Korea traveler health](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/destinations/traveler/none/south-korea)

Controlled medicines can require **advance MFDS permission regardless of nationality**. A foreign prescription alone does not establish import permission. Use the current MFDS process and confirm the medicine and quantity rather than copying old processing-time promises. [Controlled medicines brought into Korea](https://www.mfds.go.kr/eng/brd/m_60/view.do?seq=72497)

## During travel

Keep essential medication in hand luggage. On a long flight, move periodically when safe and follow any personal medical advice. Adjust sleep to the actual itinerary; the former precise jet-lag schedule and assumed total flight duration were not supported.

Pack comfortable shoes, layers, a small first-aid kit appropriate to you, and usual prescriptions. Ask a pharmacist or clinician about medicines unfamiliar to you rather than using a generic travel checklist as dosing advice.

## Food and access

Use [[Korea 2026 — Food]]: your current profile is an unrestricted diet with no reported food allergies and a preference for high-protein meals. Update the guide if that changes. Consider steep paths, steps and confined-space walking when deciding on Gamcheon, temple visits and the tunnel; ask the operator about the specific route and assistance available.

## If you need help

**Ambulance/fire: 119. Police: 112.** Provide your location and keep the Korean hotel address available. For nonurgent illness, your insurer or accommodation can help locate an appropriate clinic; that does not guarantee language support or coverage. [Seoul visitor safety](https://english.visitseoul.net/essential-Info-article/Safety/ENN012370)
