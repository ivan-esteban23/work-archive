<summary>A practical Bandcamp digging routine</summary>
	1. Open a record above and play the suggested track, then one contrasting track.
	2. Read its release date, credits, tags, and edition notes. Distinguish albums from EPs, singles, compilations, live records, remasters, and preorders.
	3. Follow the artist and label links; explore their catalogs and recommendations. Search deathrock, gothic rock, darkwave, coldwave, and post-punk as separate routes. Broad tags can return unrelated music.
	4. If a record interests you, save the direct album URL and exact edition in your listening journal. Write one sentence about atmosphere and one about rhythm or guitar/bass weight.
	5. For physical records, inspect the specific vinyl/CD/cassette listing, shipping destination, and final total before deciding. Availability and prices change.
