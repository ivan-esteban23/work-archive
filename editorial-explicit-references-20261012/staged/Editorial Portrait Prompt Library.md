## 01 — Gray Suit

![[Attachments/Editorial Portraits/01 — Gray Suit — Original.jpeg|450]]

hand against cheek, gray suit and tie, long black hair, cool studio background.

**Image reference:** [[Attachments/Editorial Portraits/01 — Gray Suit — Original.jpeg|Open reference image]]  
**Attach this file:** `01 — Gray Suit — Original.jpeg`

### Prompt

```text
REFERENCE IMAGE: 01 — Gray Suit — Original.jpeg
Attach this image with the prompt below. The filename alone does not attach the image.

Create a professional photographic fashion editorial portrait using the supplied image as the visual reference. Preserve the adult subject's recognizable facial appearance, long sleek black hair with a subtle side part, direct eye contact, calm poised expression, and hand resting gently against the cheek on the left side of the frame. Wardrobe: beautifully tailored charcoal-gray wool suit jacket, crisp white shirt with an elongated cuff, and a textured dark-gray tie. Background: seamless pale blue-gray studio backdrop, clean and unobtrusive. Composition: vertical 4:5 head-and-upper-torso portrait, carefully balanced crop with breathing room above the hair, eyes as the primary focal point, hand and tailoring clearly visible. Photographic treatment: premium beauty and fashion campaign, 85 mm portrait lens perspective, sharp eyes and lashes, subtle falloff in focus, large diffused key light slightly above and to camera left, soft fill and delicate hair separation, controlled highlights. Retouching: elegant and realistic; retain fine skin texture and believable pores, gentle rosy makeup and soft pink lips, faithful proportions, lifelike eyes, individual hair strands and visible woven fabric. Color grading: restrained cool neutrals with subtle warm skin tones, refined contrast, no excessive saturation. Anatomically correct hand and fingers, graceful manicure. Generate one polished high-resolution portrait. No text, logos, border, watermark, extra accessories, exaggerated airbrushing or artificial plastic skin.
```

## 02 — Lavender and Silver

![[Attachments/Editorial Portraits/02 — Lavender and Silver — Original.jpeg|450]]

lavender knit, pulled-back hair, two necklaces, pale blue-gray wall. The original has magazine lettering.

**Image reference:** [[Attachments/Editorial Portraits/02 — Lavender and Silver — Original.jpeg|Open reference image]]  
**Attach this file:** `02 — Lavender and Silver — Original.jpeg`

### Prompt

```text
REFERENCE IMAGE: 02 — Lavender and Silver — Original.jpeg
Attach this image with the prompt below. The filename alone does not attach the image.

SUBJECT: Young East Asian woman, mid-20s, delicate oval face, natural straight brows, minimal soft-pink lip, luminous fresh skin with visible natural texture, calm neutral gaze directly to camera. Dark hair pulled into a low ponytail at the nape, two or three loose strands framing the cheek and temple.

WARDROBE: Lilac / lavender bouclé-textured knit top with a soft V neckline, relaxed shoulder, matte fabric, no logos.

JEWELRY (hero detail): layered fine silver-toned chains — a short strand with a small round brilliant stone pendant, a longer strand with a polished crescent / horseshoe pendant; a single drop earring with a halo-set rectangular crystal, faceted sparkle catching the key light. Cool white metal, realistic chain links and prong detail.

ENVIRONMENT: Leaning against a smooth pale blue-grey painted wall, matte texture, no props.

LIGHTING: soft diffused directional daylight from camera-left, high-key, gentle wrap, minimal shadow, subtle specular highlight on cheekbone, earring and chain; faint warm bounce on the shadow side.

CAMERA & COMPOSITION: vertical 3:4, tight beauty crop, head and shoulders, face placed on the left vertical third, generous negative space to the right, subject resting at 8–10 degrees head tilt, eye line just above lens axis. Shot on 85mm f/1.8 at full-frame, shallow depth of field, wall softly rendered.

COLOR & GRADE: pastel cool palette, lilac and pale blue, low saturation, medium-low contrast, filmic highlight rolloff, subtle fine grain, clean commercial beauty retouching that keeps skin texture intact.

MOOD: quiet, intimate, effortless, understated elegance.

NEGATIVE: text, watermark, logo, caption, border, collage, split frame, oversaturation, harsh flash, HDR, plastic skin, over-smoothed skin, heavy contouring, visible retouch lines, extra fingers, distorted hands, malformed jewelry, tangled or broken chain links, mismatched earrings, blurry eyes, motion blur, busy background, clutter.

SHORT VARIANT (for models with tighter context limits)

Editorial beauty portrait of a young East Asian woman with dark hair in a low ponytail, loose face-framing strands, wearing a lilac bouclé V-neck knit and layered fine silver chains with a small stone pendant and crescent pendant, plus a halo-set rectangular crystal drop earring. Calm gaze at camera, head tilted, leaning on a pale blue-grey matte wall. Soft diffused daylight from the left, high-key, pastel cool palette, 85mm f/1.8, shallow depth of field, 3:4 vertical, fine grain, natural skin texture, no text.
```

### Prompt

```text
REFERENCE IMAGE: 06 — Cream Sofa and Ruby Red — Original.jpeg
Attach this image with the prompt below. The filename alone does not attach the image.

Create one high-luxury photographic fashion editorial using the attached reference for composition, styling, warm color palette, and relaxed attitude. Cast an original fictional adult woman in her mid-20s; use the photograph as a visual style reference rather than requiring an exact likeness.

SUBJECT & EXPRESSION: Shoulder-length to long honey-blonde hair with a soft center part, loose lived-in waves and individual flyaway strands. Direct eye contact, composed neutral expression, understated peach blush, softly defined eyes, satin rose lips. Preserve natural skin texture, believable pores and subtle tonal variation.

POSE & COMPOSITION: Seated on a generously cushioned ivory sofa, knees bent and drawn close to the torso, legs together, upper body leaning slightly forward. Both arms extend naturally downward, hands resting gently around the lower shins just above the ankles, fingers relaxed and anatomically correct. Maintain the reference's compact, balanced silhouette and frontal view. Vertical 9:16 composition with breathing room above the head and enough space below to show both shoes completely. Face remains the primary focal point; hands and red footwear form secondary points of interest.

WARDROBE & HERO ACCENT: A refined fitted sleeveless top with narrow muted red and charcoal horizontal stripes, pale gray trim and small dark button details; premium light-wash straight-leg denim with authentic cotton weave, subtle fading and natural folds at the bent knees. Glossy ruby-red patent-leather flats with slim straps and small polished gold-tone buckles, fully visible, with controlled reflections and precise construction. Short, softly polished pale-pink nails. Understated styling, no added jewelry or visible branding.

ENVIRONMENT: Quiet private salon in an elegant contemporary residence; sumptuous ivory upholstery, oversized cream cushions and warm matte sand-colored plaster walls with a subtle vertical seam. Restrained set dressing, tactile natural materials, uncluttered background. Keep the casual intimacy of the reference while elevating upholstery, tailoring and photographic finish.

LIGHTING: Reinterpret the reference's warm direct-light character through a professionally controlled near-axis key with gentle diffusion, softly feathered warm fill and subtle ambient room light. Luminous skin, gentle directional shadows behind the subject, clean catchlights and carefully shaped reflections on the patent leather and buckles. Warm late-afternoon atmosphere without a heavy orange cast, clipped highlights or flash hotspots; retain detail in cream cushions and blonde hair.

CAMERA & FINISH: 50mm full-frame perspective, camera positioned near the seated subject's eye level, sufficient working distance to avoid enlarging knees or hands. Moderate depth of field, approximately f/4, keeping the eyes, hands, denim and footwear clearly resolved while gently softening the wall. Premium fashion campaign photography, meticulous material detail, restrained editorial retouching, smooth highlight rolloff, subtle fine film grain. Palette of ivory, sand, honey blonde and pale denim anchored by ruby red; balanced warm skin, elegant contrast and realistic saturation.

MOOD: Effortless private-residence luxury, confident ease, intimate fashion editorial, polished yet unposed.

AVOID: Text, logos, watermark, borders, collage, decorative clutter, added accessories, exaggerated glamour makeup, plastic skin, excessive orange grading, HDR halos, distorted perspective, enlarged knees, elongated arms, extra or fused fingers, malformed feet, mismatched shoes, broken buckles, cropped footwear, smeared denim texture, blurry eyes, artificial hair, cartoon or illustration.
```
