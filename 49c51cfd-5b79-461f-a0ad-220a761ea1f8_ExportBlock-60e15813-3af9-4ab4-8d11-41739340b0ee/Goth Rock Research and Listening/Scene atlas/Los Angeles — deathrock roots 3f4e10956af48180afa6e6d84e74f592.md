## Context

LA Weekly’s 2009 account places Christian Death, 45 Grave, and Kommunity FK within the local deathrock story. [Scene account](https://www.laweekly.com/the-la-deathrock-starter-guide/).

## Listening anchors

- Only Theatre of Pain — Christian Death
- Sleep in Safety — 45 Grave
- The Vision and the Voice — Kommunity FK

## Listen through the scene

Compare the three dossiers through rhythm, guitar texture, and voice. A shared scene does not establish one uniform sound.
