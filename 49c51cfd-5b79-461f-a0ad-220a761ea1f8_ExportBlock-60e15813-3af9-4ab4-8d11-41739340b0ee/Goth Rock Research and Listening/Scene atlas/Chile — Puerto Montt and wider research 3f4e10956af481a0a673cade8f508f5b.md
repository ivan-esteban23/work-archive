## Context

A December 2024 specialist feature locates A Través del Manto in Puerto Montt and discusses Xmal Funeral. Its praise and stylistic comparisons have a promotional tone. [Feature](https://post-punk.com/chilean-gothic-rockers-a-traves-del-manto-unveil-their-macabre-video-for-xmal-funeral/).

## Listening anchors

- A Través del Manto — Xmal Funeral, single; direct artist link below
- Diavol Strâin — “11 Ecos” and the *Eterno Retorno* LP (2026)
- Gemidos Necrofílicos — *Las Ruinas del Alma*, for the deathrock side of the scene

## Listen through the scene

Compare rhythmic drive and atmosphere with your named references. “Death Goth” is an artist description here, not an established universal category. Self-described genres across these bands span gothic rock, deathrock, darkwave, coldwave, and shoegaze-adjacent textures — Chile has no single documented sound.

## Documented Chilean bands

Verified 2026-10-09 against artist-owned pages where marked. Self-descriptions are quoted from the artists' own pages; scene narrative comes from attributed editorial sources.

- **Diavol Strâin** — Valparaíso/Santiago; self-described “Riot Dark Wave.” Releases include *Eterno Retorno* (LP, 2026), *Elegía del Olvido / Elegía del Horror* (LP, 2021), and *Todo El Caos Habita Aquí* (LP, 2018). [Bandcamp](https://diavolstrain.bandcamp.com/) · A [post-punk.com feature](https://post-punk.com/in-a-vile-delirium-chilean-darkwavers-diavol-strain-unveil-a-haunted-tableau-with-video-for-11-ecos) calls the members “bona fide icons of Chilean and Latin American darkwave” (attributed opinion).
- **Gemidos Necrofílicos** — Coquimbo; formed 2008; described on its own page as “one of the most representative Deathrock and Post-Punk bands of the north of the country.” Releases include *Las Ruinas del Alma* and *Delirium Trémens*. [Bandcamp](https://gemidosnecrofilicos.bandcamp.com/)
- **Sweet Spectra** — Santiago; gothic rock duo formed mid-2014 (Nathalie, bass/vocals; NIC.H, guitars); *Seven Visions* (LP, 2018); played Tumbas Eternas Fest (Lima, 2018) and Deepland Fest II (São Paulo, 2018). [Bandcamp](https://sweetspectraoficial.bandcamp.com/)
- **Seatemples** — self-described “dark cinematic layers… post-punk, goth rock, shoegaze, darkwave textures”; *Trópicos* (LP, 2020; CD via Icy Cold Records, France; cassette via Blackjack Illuminist Records, Berlin). [Bandcamp](https://seatemples.bandcamp.com/) · [post-punk.com feature, 2021](https://post-punk.com/chilean-shoegaze-infused-post-punk-outfit-seatemples-debut-video-for-weltschmerz)
- **Delirium Mask** — Santiago; formed mid-2018; self-described “gótico, postpunk y punk siniestro.” [Bandcamp](https://deliriummask.bandcamp.com/)
- **Amöniacö** — formed 2008; *Azul Prusia* (EP, 2019, Cleopatra Records); a [post-punk.com feature](https://post-punk.com/chilean-darkwave-act-amoniaco-return-with-the-soaring-and-melancholic-ritos) says Robert Smith selected the band to support The Cure's Chile show in 2013 (attributed opinion).
- **Luna In Cælo** — Santiago; project begun 1993; ethereal/ambient direction; *Miedo A Morir* (2000) and *Urdimbre* (2006). [Bandcamp](https://lunaincaelo.bandcamp.com/)
- **Ángel De La Guarda** — Santiago; *Alfanumérico* (LP, 2023); included in Remezcla's Spanish-language goth list. [Bandcamp](https://angeldelaguarda.bandcamp.com/) · [Remezcla list](https://remezcla.com/lists/music/vampiros-10-bands-biting-into-goth-darkwave-en-espanol)
- **Vioflesh** — Santiago; self-described “darkwave and neo-coldwave duo”; “Call Me” (single, 4 September 2026, darkTunes Music Group). [Bandcamp](https://vioflesh.bandcamp.com/)
- **Binzatina** — Santiago; self-defined “Trip-Goth”; *Anestesia* (2018), *Camaleón* (2022). [Bandcamp](https://binzatina.bandcamp.com/)
- **Holograma** — minimal coldwave; self-titled EP (2022). City conflicts: Bandcamp reads Santiago, gothdb reads Rancagua. [Bandcamp](https://hologramacold.bandcamp.com/)
- **Poluto** — Santiago; post-punk/darkwave with reggae and new wave influences. [Bandcamp](https://poluto.bandcamp.com/)

Secondary-only leads (no artist-owned page verified): Los Dosis (Talcahuano/Concepción, gothic rock/post-punk, per gothdb); Ultrastigma (deathrock, per El Claustro, September 2026); BlindStar (post-punk trio, per El Claustro, September 2026). Treat these as leads, not verified discographies.

## Labels and promoters

- **El Claustro** — Chilean non-profit label and promoter for “gothic, dark, underground” music, distributing digitally via Boost Collective; its running Chile coverage is the best scene index found. [Label and promotion](https://www.elclaustro.cl/label-promotion/) · [Chile tag index](https://www.elclaustro.cl/tag/chile/)
- **Surpop Records** — Concepción-based independent label; no goth acts verified on its catalog as of the check date. [Website](https://surpoprecords.com/)
- European labels releasing Chilean acts: Swiss Dark Nights (A Través del Manto), Cleopatra Records (Amöniacö), Icy Cold Records and Blackjack Illuminist Records (Seatemples physical editions), darkTunes Music Group and Rayón Records (recent singles). These are export routes, not Chilean labels.
- Print: *Ruido Interior: Radiografía de la escena gótica en Chile* (César Mancilla Salazar) documents the scene and claims an appendix of 200+ bands; pre-sale closed 13 September 2026 with printing scheduled mid-September 2026 — the printed book was not verified as of the check date. [El Claustro, August 2026](https://www.elclaustro.cl/ruido-interior-la-radiografia-definitiva-de-la-escena-gotica-chilena-llega-en-edicion-limitada/)

## Open research

Build outward using Chilean interviews, venues, local labels, and original recordings. One band does not stand for Chile or Latin America. [Artist single](https://atravesdelmanto.bandcamp.com/track/xmal-funeral-2).

- Puerto Montt still has one documented band (A Través del Manto); no second band, venue, or scene report was found.
- Concepción/Biobío and Valparaíso each have only one lead (Los Dosis; Diavol Strâin).
- No Chilean goth-specific physical-catalog label was verified; El Claustro is digital-only and Surpop's catalog has no verified goth acts.
- Conflicts kept visible: Holograma's city (Santiago vs Rancagua); Binzatina's formation year (2015 vs 2017).
- Las Eras and Euroshima are Argentine acts frequently on Chilean bills — not Chilean bands.
