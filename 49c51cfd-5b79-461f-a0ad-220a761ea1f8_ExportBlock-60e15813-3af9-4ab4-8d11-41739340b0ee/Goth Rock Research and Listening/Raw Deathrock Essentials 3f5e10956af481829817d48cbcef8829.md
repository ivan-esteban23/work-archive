# 01 / THE RAW FILES

*From Los Angeles to the contemporary underground. Twenty recordings, five chapters, one listening journey.*

---

<aside>
🩸

Twenty-record exploratory path. No listens or scores inferred. Original-year and edition details should be checked in linked dossiers before ranking.

</aside>

## First wave · Los Angeles and US roots

1. 45 Grave — Sleep in Safety (1983). Listen for punk attack and horror-theatre imagery.
2. Kommunity FK — The Vision and the Voice (1983). Track bass and rhythm.
3. Christian Death — Only Theatre of Pain (1982). Compare guitar abrasion and vocal delivery.
4. Super Heroines — Cry for Help (1982). Listen for raw guitar urgency.
5. Voodoo Church — Voodoo Church (1982 EP). Distinguish EP from album.

## UK and European connections

1. Ausgang — The Teachings of Web (1984). Check original format and release edition.
2. Sex Gang Children — Song and Legend (1983). Focus on theatrical voice.
3. Specimen — Batastrophe (1983 EP). A Batcave-related EP, not a studio LP.
4. Alien Sex Fiend — Who's Been Sleeping in My Brain? (1983). Compare electronic texture.
5. Xmal Deutschland — Fetisch (1983). Gothic post-punk neighbor, not automatically deathrock.

## Later developments

1. Cinema Strange — Cinema Strange (2000). Compare theatricality with early bands.
2. Bloody Dead and Sexy — Paint It Red (2003). Test punk energy.
3. Scarlet's Remains — The Palest Grey (2005). Listen for bass and vocal tension.
4. Christ vs. Warhol — Dissent (2010). Compare rhythmic attack.
5. Altar de Fey — Echoes in the Corridor (2015). Trace early-to-later continuity.

## Modern exploratory leads

1. Horror Vacui — In Darkness You Will Feel Alright (2012). Test driving rhythms.
2. Bat Nouveau — Metamorphoses (2014). Check atmosphere against attack.
3. Tears for the Dying — In the Shadow of the Midnight Sun (2023). Compare punk edge and melody.
4. Illegal Funeral — Scold’s Bridle (2026). Artist-linked current research.
5. Normal Weirds — Frown Time (2026). Compare deathpunk to classic deathrock.
