## Bass and rhythmic grip

- Follow repeated bass ostinatos, note attack and interplay with kick/snare.
- Compare The Cure's A Forest, The Sisters of Mercy's Alice and 45 Grave's Evil.

## Guitars

- Listen for chorus, flanging, delay, reverb, distortion and dry rhythmic attacks.
- Compare the layered space on The Cure's Pornography with sharper deathrock guitar attacks.

## Drums and machines

- Contrast live drum dynamics with the programmed pulse associated with The Sisters of Mercy.
- Do not infer drum machine model without a credit or interview.

## Vocals

- Compare Rozz Williams's theatrical delivery, Andrew Eldritch's low register and Cinema Strange's character-led phrasing.
- Describe phrasing, pitch, reverb and urgency separately.

## Production and space

- Reverb: decay and perceived distance.
- Delay: discrete repetitions.
- Chorus: modulated doubling effect.
- Flanger: sweeping comb-filter effect.
- Compression: dynamic range and perceived punch.

## Three-question session

1. What drives the rhythm?
2. Which instrument makes the atmosphere?
3. What moment stays in memory?
