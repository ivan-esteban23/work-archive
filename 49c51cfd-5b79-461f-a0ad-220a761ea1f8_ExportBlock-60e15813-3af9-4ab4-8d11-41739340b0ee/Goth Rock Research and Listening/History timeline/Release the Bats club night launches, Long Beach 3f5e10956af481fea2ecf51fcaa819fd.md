# Release the Bats club night launches, Long Beach

Date precision: Exact date
Kind: Club / scene
Scene: Los Angeles
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/deathrock
Window start: 31 de octubre de 1998
Year: 1998

The Long Beach club night launched on Halloween 1998 and ran for twenty years. Wikipedia and Souciant date the first deathrock revival to its founding; it is the documented bridge from the 1980s scene into the 2000s.
