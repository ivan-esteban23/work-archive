# World Goth Day originates on BBC Radio 6 Music

Date precision: Exact date
Kind: Scene milestone
Scene: UK
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Goth_Day
Window start: 22 de mayo de 2009
Year: 2009

A "Goth Day" broadcast on BBC Radio 6 Music (Cruel Britannia / Martin OldGoth) on 22 May 2009 became the origin of World Goth Day, now observed internationally — including in Chile, as the scene atlas records.
