## 01 / SHROUDS — Grimoire {color="purple"}
![Shrouds — Grimoire. Release artwork from the artist’s Bandcamp.](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0143441930_10.jpg)
**The route:** follow the historical deathrock features toward a contemporary act with documented Southern Californian connections.
The band’s profile lists Leipzig and Southern California, with activity beginning in 2017. Grimoire was released April 7, 2023; its eight tracks include Mercy, Circles and Initiation. Ana Thema handles vocals/guitar, Grimm Beatz percussion/backing vocals, and Ludwig Wilde bass. These are the artist’s current profile credits rather than an audited recording-session lineup. [Artist release and biography](https://shrouds-official.bandcamp.com/album/grimoire).
I Die: You Die’s June 2023 write-up hears a rough, economical deathrock approach in Mercy and compares it with Altar De Fey, TSOL and March Violets. This is the publication’s assessment, offered as a hypothesis to test. [Independent track commentary](https://www.idieyoudie.com/2023/06/12/tracks-june-12th-2023/).
**Start here:** [Mercy](https://shrouds-official.bandcamp.com/track/mercy) → [Circles](https://shrouds-official.bandcamp.com/track/circles) → the complete record.
**Listening question:** does a compact arrangement give each instrument more character? Name the part that keeps drawing your attention.
**Edition note:** the album page also lists physical editions. A vinyl preorder’s June 2026 shipping estimate is separate from the 2023 release date; it does not establish a new album or completed shipment. [Edition listing](https://shrouds-official.bandcamp.com/album/grimoire).

## 02 / EAT MY TEETH — Eat My Teeth {color="purple"}
![EAT MY TEETH — Eat My Teeth. Cover art credited to Natt Nemesism on the artist’s release page.](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3993701763_10.jpg)
**The route:** test whether the punk side of the magazine matters as much to you as its theatrical side.
The Warsaw band describes itself as anarcho-deathrock. Its self-titled seven-track release is dated June 24, 2026, with cover art by Natt Nemesism. Earlier catalog entries reach back to 2022, so this is not presented as the band’s first release. [Artist release and catalog](https://eatmyteeth.bandcamp.com/album/eat-my-teeth).
**Start here:** [Rot](https://eatmyteeth.bandcamp.com/track/rot) → [Eat My Teeth](https://eatmyteeth.bandcamp.com/track/eat-my-teeth) → the complete release.
**Listening question:** compare what makes you replay the shorter track with what holds your attention in the longer one. Is it an instrumental figure, a vocal gesture, or the way the parts change?
**Edition note:** Bandcamp’s “Digital Album” purchase heading does not resolve EP versus album terminology. The page says a physical release is forthcoming without specifying its format or date. No independent review is used here; genre attribution comes from the band. [Release details](https://eatmyteeth.bandcamp.com/album/eat-my-teeth).

## 03 / DOSES NOCIVAS — Liberum Es {color="purple"}
![Doses Nocivas — Liberum Es. Artwork from the artist’s Bandcamp; designer credit not supplied there.](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2375067079_10.jpg)
**The route:** widen the magazine’s South American coverage with a short, documented release.
The Brazilian band says it formed in 2023 and draws on classic and modern post-punk. Liberum Es is a four-track digital release dated June 21, 2025. The profile names Maicon Douglas on vocals/guitar/synth, Flavio Felix on vocals/bass, and Jo Oliveira on drums. Its tags include post-punk, goth and darkwave; those tags do not establish deathrock classification. [Artist release and biography](https://dosesnocivas.bandcamp.com/album/liberum-es).
**Start here:** [Quem Sou](https://dosesnocivas.bandcamp.com/track/quem-sou) → [Lacrimis Rigare Flores](https://dosesnocivas.bandcamp.com/track/lacrimis-rigare-flores) → all four tracks.
**Listening question:** where do guitar, bass and synth sit in your attention? Decide whether this expands your preferred sound or points toward a separate listening lane.
**Open research:** the page does not establish a city, physical edition or artwork designer. Keep those fields open. This card uses artist evidence rather than an independent critical verdict.
