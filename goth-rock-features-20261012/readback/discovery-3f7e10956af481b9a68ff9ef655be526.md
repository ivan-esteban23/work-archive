## A revival with a theatrical identity {color="purple"}
Liz Ohanesian’s 2009 LA Weekly guide connects Cinema Strange’s late-1990s Los Angeles club activity to a sound she compares with Sex Gang Children and Virgin Prunes. Her account follows the band into a German-label relationship and Wave-Gotik-Treffen. This is a historical scene portrait, not evidence of present touring activity. [LA Weekly — scene guide](https://www.laweekly.com/the-la-deathrock-starter-guide/).
[Post-Punk.com](http://Post-Punk.com)’s 2022 retrospective identifies Lucas Lanthier and the Ribiat brothers as the core trio and regards the self-titled debut as a catalyst for the deathrock/Batcave revival. Attribute that influence judgment to the publication; it is not a universal verdict. [Post-Punk.com — debut retrospective](https://post-punk.com/cinema-stranges-debut-album-released-on-vinyl-for-the-first-time/).
## Two albums, two different starting points {color="purple"}
The self-titled debut gathered earlier material: the 1996 Acrobat Amaranth Automaton EP and late-1990s singles, according to the same retrospective. That helps explain the member catalog’s description of The Astonished Eyes of Evening as the second Trisol CD but the first full-length recording project. Those statements describe release position and working process, respectively. [Post-Punk.com](https://post-punk.com/cinema-stranges-debut-album-released-on-vinyl-for-the-first-time/) · [Member catalog](https://lucaslanthier.bandcamp.com/album/album-the-astonished-eyes-of-evening).
**The issue’s comparison route uses The Astonished Eyes of Evening (2002).** The monthly desk’s Cinema Strange (2000) is a separate debut-album exploration. The Wax Moon vinyl release discussed in 2022 belongs to the debut, not to Astonished Eyes. [Post-Punk.com](https://post-punk.com/cinema-stranges-debut-album-released-on-vinyl-for-the-first-time/).
## Inside The Astonished Eyes of Evening {color="purple"}
The member catalog credits Lanthier with voices and programming, Michael Ribiat with guitars, organ and piano, and Daniel Ribiat with bass, keyboards and omnichord. Ashkelon Sain and the band produced it; recording and mixing took place mainly January–August 2001, with Speak, Marauder! recorded in October 2000. The credits make the arrangements worth investigating without proving which instrument appears at every moment. [Member catalog and credits](https://lucaslanthier.bandcamp.com/album/album-the-astonished-eyes-of-evening).
The contemporary [metal.de](http://metal.de) review, dated 12 March 2002, argues that the band made a more complicated, less immediately accessible second record and rewards its bizarre atmosphere. Its 8/10 is that outlet’s editorial rating, not your score. Its broad Gothic Metal/Mittelalter category is a website classification, not sufficient evidence to recast Cinema Strange as gothic metal. [metal.de — contemporary review](https://www.metal.de/reviews/cinema-strange-the-astonished-eyes-of-evening-2094/).
## Three listening scenes {color="purple"}
These questions use the verified track identities as a proposed listening route. They do not claim direct audio analysis.
- **Dead Eyes Open:** follow vocal character and instrumental repetition separately. Is theatrical delivery a hook for you, or does it pull attention away from the music?
- **Catacomb Kittens:** use the longer form to test sustained interest. What makes you want another section: a repeated figure, a change in voice, or a narrative question?
- **Legs and Tarpaulin:** the artist identifies Poe’s King Pest as the lyrical source. Compare that confirmed reference with the character you hear; do not assume every other song is also a Poe adaptation. [Artist lyrics and notes](https://lucaslanthier.bandcamp.com/album/album-the-astonished-eyes-of-evening).
## Literature, credits and interpretation
The catalog also identifies a Scriabin prelude as the source for the end of Tomb Lilies. These two explicit references make literature and composition useful research paths. Other apparent cinematic or literary parallels remain interpretation until supported by an artist statement. [Member notes](https://lucaslanthier.bandcamp.com/album/album-the-astonished-eyes-of-evening).
