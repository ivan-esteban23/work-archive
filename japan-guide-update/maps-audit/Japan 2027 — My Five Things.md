## 🧭 When two plans compete

1. Which one supports a priority I have not experienced yet?
2. Is the opportunity time-sensitive, such as good blossoms or light?
3. Does it fit today's energy, route, and remaining budget?

Choose the better fit and let the other option go. Rest can be the best decision, too.
