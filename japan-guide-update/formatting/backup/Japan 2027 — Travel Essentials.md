## 📱 Visit Japan Web — prepare before flying

**Checked 6 October 2026; recheck terminal arrangements for 2027.** Online preparation reduces paperwork; it does not guarantee a short queue or replace immigration inspection.

1. Open [the official Visit Japan Web landing page](https://services.digital.go.jp/en/visit-japan-web/) and create an account.
2. Enter your traveler/passport information and the actual arrival flight and first accommodation details.
3. Complete the immigration and customs declarations and have the resulting arrival QR/2D code ready with your passport.
4. Keep the phone charged and save an offline copy of the code as a backup. Update the registration if flight or accommodation details change.

Visit Japan Web supports online arrival procedures. [Digital Agency explanation](https://www.digital.go.jp/en/policies/visit_japan_web)

### What happens after landing

Where integrated kiosks operate, scan your code and passport, complete the photo/fingerprint steps as instructed, and follow the displayed routing to the immigration booth. Collect luggage, then complete customs; eligible passengers may use a facial-recognition customs gate, while others are directed to an officer.

**Current integrated-kiosk locations relevant to your trip:** Haneda terminals **2 and 3**; Narita terminal **3**. The published list does not establish integrated-kiosk availability at Narita terminals 1 or 2. Check your actual arrival terminal and follow staff instructions. [Official terminal list](https://www.customs.go.jp/english/passenger/kiosk_2.pdf)

Integrated kiosks reduce duplicate data submission; dedicated immigration booths still involve inspection. Customs may require additional checks. **Do not plan an airport transfer around a guaranteed processing time.** [Official integrated-kiosk process](https://www.customs.go.jp/english/passenger/Pilot_kiosk.htm)

## 🧳 Tokyo–Kyoto luggage decision

Measure **length + width + height**, including protruding handles and wheels, before buying the train ticket.

| Suitcase total dimensions | Current JR Central guidance for ordinary luggage |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 160 cm | Oversized-baggage reservation is not required by size; storage must suit the bag and available space |
| Over 160 cm and up to 250 cm | Reserve a seat with an oversized baggage area |
| Over 250 cm | Cannot be brought aboard under these rules; arrange another solution |

JR Central’s summary distinguishes these size bands. Selecting an ordinary reserved seat does not automatically reserve the oversized area. Recheck the current operator rules and any special-item exceptions before booking. ## 📱 Phone and payment backups

These are practical preparation suggestions; test them with your actual devices, bank, and mobile provider.

- [ ] Confirm the phone is unlocked and supports the selected eSIM/SIM; check activation timing, data allowance, and hotspot terms before paying.
- [ ] Decide how to receive bank verification codes abroad; a data-only travel eSIM may not receive calls or SMS sent to your home number. Check roaming charges with your home carrier.
- [ ] Carry a backup physical payment card separately from the main wallet, plus some yen. Confirm overseas use and know the card-freezing procedure.
- [ ] Save hotel addresses in Japanese, confirmations, key contacts, and a simple route offline. Do not rely entirely on live maps or a phone wallet.
- [ ] Prepare secure account-recovery access if the phone is lost; keep recovery codes private and separate from the device.

**If the phone or wallet is lost:** contact the venue/transport operator and local police as appropriate; freeze affected cards and use the device’s lost-mode controls from a trusted device. Use the hotel and [[#📞 Contacts to save offline|contacts card]] for assistance.

## 🎁 Bringing matcha and food souvenirs back to Chile

**Official guidance checked 6 October 2026; recheck before returning in 2027.** SAG requires declaration of plant- and animal-origin products carried into Chile. Declare tea/matcha and relevant food souvenirs; commercial or sealed packaging does not remove the declaration obligation. An inspector determines whether entry is permitted. [SAG traveler declaration guidance](https://www.sag.gob.cl/tramites/declaracion-jurada-sag-sobre-ingreso-de-productos-de-origen-vegetal-o-animal-chile) · [SAG entry rules](https://www.sag.gob.cl/ambitos-de-accion/ingreso-chile)

- [ ] Check the specific products with SAG before buying anything whose admission is uncertain.
- [ ] Keep tea and food in original labeled packaging and accessible for inspection.
- [ ] Prepare the required declaration through [SAG’s official portal](https://dj.sag.gob.cl/) or the accepted arrival procedure; do not guess the opening window for 2027.
