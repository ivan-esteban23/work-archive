## Start at the correct Tokyo airport

**Haneda (HND), mainland rail route:** follow your terminal's signs to **Keikyu**, choose the service toward **Shinagawa**, and change there for your onward train. For Tokyo Station, continue on JR from Shinagawa. Some Keikyu services have different destinations or require a change; check the departure display. [Official Tokyo Haneda access guide](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/plan/airport-access/haneda-airport/).

**Narita (NRT), mainland rail route:** compare **Narita Express to Tokyo or Shinagawa**, selecting the service that calls at your onward station. For a Tokyo hotel, choose the airport route by the hotel's actual nearest station instead of assuming Shinagawa is best. [JR East Narita Express](https://www.jreast.co.jp/en/multi/nex/).

**Continuing by domestic flight:** clear the international-arrival procedures, collect/handle baggage as instructed, reach the correct domestic terminal/check-in area, and clear domestic security. Use the airline's required check-in and connection times, including terminal changes. [Haneda international-to-domestic guide](https://tokyo-haneda.com/en/flight/transit/int_dms.html) · [Narita international-to-domestic guide](https://www.narita-airport.jp/en/airportguide/connect/inter-dom/) · [Narita terminal shuttle](https://www.narita-airport.jp/en/access/shuttlebus/).

**Haneda and Narita are separate airports.** If an onward flight leaves the other airport, price and schedule the ground transfer explicitly. A Tokyo airport code on a search result does not establish a same-airport connection. For separate tickets, check who handles a missed connection, baggage charges, and cancellation terms before choosing the itinerary.

For your existing Tokyo-first trip, my recommendation is **airport → Tokyo hotel → island outing later**. A long island transfer immediately after the international arrival is an alternative only when the actual landing time and connections make it practical.

## Where this fits your itinerary

- **Enoshima:** start from your Tokyo hotel, replace another Tokyo outing, and return to the same room.
- **Miyajima / Naoshima / Shodoshima:** choose a western-Japan block around Kyoto/Osaka rather than return to the Tokyo airport just to reach the island. The island chapter has explicit 14-night Miyajima/Naoshima alternatives.
- **Yakushima / Ishigaki / Miyako:** compare a domestic-flight module with its own arrival, departure, and weather buffer; rebuild mainland nights when selected.
- **Taketomi:** use Ishigaki as the access base; choose the ferry day after checking actual services.
- **Sado:** use a northern-route module via Niigata, replacing other nights rather than adding an uncounted detour.
