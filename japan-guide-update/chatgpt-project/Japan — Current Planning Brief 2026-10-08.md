## Prepare now, whatever month you choose

1. **Documents:** check passport nationality, type and expiry for each traveler. MOFA currently lists Chile for eligible short stays, normally 90 days, but leaving from Chile does not establish your nationality. Transit eligibility follows the actual connection route. [[Japan 2027 — Travel Essentials#🛂 Visa check — passport confirmation pending|Existing checklist]] · [MOFA visa exemptions](https://www.mofa.go.jp/j_info/visit/visa/short/novisa.html).
2. **Medicines:** make a private list of active ingredients and quantities. MHLW permits up to one month's supply of many ordinary prescription medicines without Import Confirmation; controlled ingredients have separate rules. Some medicines are prohibited despite a foreign prescription. Where controlled-drug permission applies, NCD says to apply at least 14 days before travel; start earlier to resolve questions. [MHLW ordinary medicines](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/kenkou_iryou/iyakuhin/kojinyunyu/topics/tp010401-1_00001.html) · [NCD controlled medicines](https://www.ncd.mhlw.go.jp/en/application2.html).
3. **Phone and payments:** check eSIM compatibility, bank verification abroad, backup card/cash and offline access to the vault. Practice downloading maps and translation languages now; test the actual functions offline. Use [[Japan 2027 — Travel Essentials#📱 Phone and payment backups|the existing device checklist]]. These are preparation tasks, not claims that your setup has been tested.
4. **Walking and camera routine:** test shoes, comfortable carrying weight and a rain cover; establish a simple daily photo-backup routine. Keep planned outings modest enough for breaks. Your existing [[Japan 2027 — Photography and Filming Wishlist|photo/film wishlist]] can be reused in any season, with local access and light checked later.
5. **Insurance:** compare medical assistance, existing-condition exclusions, disruption terms and cover for the activities you actually choose, especially hiking or marine outings. JNTO recommends adequate travel insurance and provides visitor emergency guidance. [JNTO safety guidance](https://faq.japan-travel.jnto.go.jp/en/plan/emergencies/) · [[Japan 2027 — Health]].
6. **Luggage:** measure your bag and decide whether you prefer carrying or forwarding between bases. Forwarding is an available option, but hotel acceptance, delivery timing and price need confirmation for your route. Keep essentials with you while a bag is in transit. [JNTO luggage and transport guidance](https://www.japan.travel/en/responsible-travel-guide/features/traveling-comfortably/) · [[Japan 2027 — Travel Essentials#🧳 Tokyo–Kyoto luggage decision|Existing rail baggage checklist]].

## Costs and procedures worth knowing early

**Tax-free shopping after 1 November 2026:** NTA's announced refund method has eligible visitors pay tax-inclusive prices first, obtain customs confirmation that the goods are leaving Japan within 90 days of purchase, then receive the refund from the business. Carry enough funds for the gross purchase and check each retailer's refund terms. The 90 days is a customs-confirmation deadline, not a promise that a refund arrives within 90 days. [NTA April 2025 reform leaflet](https://www.nta.go.jp/publication/pamph/shohi/menzei/202506/pdf/0025006-106.pdf) · [[Japan Travel Guide 2027#🧾 Tax-free shopping for the 2027 trip|Existing detailed shopping guidance]].

**International departure tax:** the tourism agency states **JPY 3,000 per departure from Japan from 1 July 2026**, generally collected through the ticket. Transitional treatment and exemptions exist. Check the ticket breakdown and count it once; the guide's international airfare sits outside the in-Japan envelope. This is separate from a shopping-tax refund. [Japan Tourism Agency departure-tax leaflet](https://www.mlit.go.jp/kankocho/content/001996466.pdf).
