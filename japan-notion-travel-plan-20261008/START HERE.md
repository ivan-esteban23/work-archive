## What the itinerary means

The 15 days derive from the existing 25 March–8 April 2027 blossom example (14 nights: 6 Tokyo + 6 Kyoto + 2 Tokyo). Those dates are not selected, so they are deliberately absent from `Fecha`. Spring photo blocks and the day order require review after choosing season, length and flights. The departure flight from Santiago belongs on its actual local date once booked, which may differ from the Japan arrival date.

Optional city chapters are research alternatives, not extra stops added to this route. Uji, Nara, Osaka and staying in Kyoto compete for the same excursion day. Nothing in this pack is a reservation or a current availability check.
