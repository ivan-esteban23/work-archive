about

NOX NOVACULA have shared their highly-anticipated album, 'Feed The Fire' with the world via Artoffact Records. With an exciting exclusive premiere in Decibel Magazine, the band's latest output is nothing short of delectable. Bending genres, with death-rock and post-punk at the forefront, danceable sonics are present throughout and are medicine for the unrelenting soul, delivered with a gothic shine.

"We are so pleased to finally be releasing Feed the Fire into the wild. Writing and recording this album has been both a joyful and cathartic experience, and we think the result is a testament to the work we’ve done and our growth as a band. We feel so lucky to have been able to work with the exceptionally talented Matt Bayles, who recorded our first album Ascension, and who once again brought these songs to life and made them sound phenomenal.

We are also incredibly grateful for the opportunity to put out Feed the Fire as our first release with Artoffact records, a label we have highly respected for a long time, we’re honored to be a part of their roster alongside some of our favorite contemporaries." – Nox Novacula

about

Nox Novacula Seattle, Washington

NOX NOVACULA is a deathrock band formed in Seattle in 2017. Since their inception, the group has demonstrated a strong commitment to dark, gothic modes of musical expression. While homage is certainly (and devoutly) paid to their 1980s predecessors, a distinctly modern approach to personal and political struggle places NOX NOVACULA in a category of their own. ... more
