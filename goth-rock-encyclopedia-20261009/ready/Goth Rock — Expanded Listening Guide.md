# Goth Rock — Expanded Listening Guide

Research checked **9 October 2026**. Added manually to both the live Notion project and this local library; the two versions do not synchronize automatically.

**28 additional original studio albums**, organized by period and listening purpose. Together with the 11 existing dossier albums, the gallery contains 39 albums. The separate 2026 Bandcamp discovery shelf remains available in the listening room. This is an editorial exploration library, not a ranking or a record of albums you have heard.

## Start with three

For your raw-deathrock interest: **UK Decay — For Madmen Only → Sex Gang Children — Song and Legend → Nox Novacula — Feed the Fire**. This route moves from a punk-rooted historical comparison to theatrical arrangements and a newer deathrock experiment. Keep 45 Grave, Kommunity FK, and Cinema Strange as your existing reference route.

For the larger guitar sound: **The Mission — God’s Own Medicine → Fields of the Nephilim — Dawnrazor → Rosetta Stone — An Eye for the Main Chance**. For atmosphere: **Cocteau Twins — Treasure → Faith and the Muse — Elyria → Drab Majesty — The Demonstration**.

## How to read the records

The year is the original album year. Linked pages may describe later editions or archival boxes. A catalog/history page is labeled as such and may require following its listening link. Personal listening status stays unknown. The questions are suggestions, not findings from direct audio access. Twenty dossiers include authentic artwork or edition product images downloaded from their official source pages. The remaining eight retain source links while separate cover selection remains open.
