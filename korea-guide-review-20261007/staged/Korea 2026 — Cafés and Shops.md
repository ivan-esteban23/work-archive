## Sources checked

**BOAN 1942 / Boan Books, Seoul:** the official city visitor listing identifies the cultural complex, bookstore and café at **33 Hyoja-ro, Jongno-gu**, in Tongui-dong/Seochon. It lists **12:00–18:00, closed Mondays** (listing updated 22 July 2026). This is a useful candidate for 28 October after the palace; confirm the specific bookshop's hours before travel. [BOAN 1942 visitor listing](https://english.visitseoul.net/area/boan-1942/ENPk1cyet)

**Waveon Coffee, Busan:** the operator lists **286 Haemaji-ro, Jangan-eup, Gijang-gun**. It is an eastern coastal excursion, not a vintage villa next to Gwangalli. Keep it off the default Gamcheon–Gwangalli day. No current price or trip-date opening guarantee was verified. [Waveon official location](https://m.waveoncoffee.com/layout/info.html)
