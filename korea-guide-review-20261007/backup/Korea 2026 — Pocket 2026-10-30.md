# 30 October 2026 · Seoul to Gyeongju — and the reason you stopped

[[Korea 2026 — Daily Pocket Cards|All days]] · [[Korea 2026 — Pocket 2026-10-29|← Previous]] · [[Korea 2026 — Pocket 2026-10-31|Next →]]

**Base:** Gyeongju hanok, **inside Hwangnidan-gil** · night 4 · **Hotel:** TBD · **Plan:** provisional

**Density:** 🟢 Medium. One train, one transfer, and the best hour of the trip after dark.

**Main stop:** Wolji Pond and Woljeongyo Bridge, lit, at 20:30.

**Route:** Check out → Line 2 to **Seoul Station** → KTX 10:30 (2h00–2h15, ₩44,500) → bus **50 / 51 / 70** from the station, ₩1,500, ~30 min → drop bags → Daereungwon → Hwangnidan-gil → Cheomseongdae → dinner → Woljeongyo then Wolji Pond.

[Daereungwon Maps search](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=대릉원+경주) · [[Korea 2026 — Maps|Other stops]]

> ⚠️ **Check the station name on your ticket.** Current data puts the KTX at **경주역**; older guides say 신경주역. Either way the town is ~12 km out — bus ₩1,500 or taxi ₩15,000. Allow the transfer.

**Food / café:** Lunch *Hwangnam Gukbap* — **sarangchae**, nine to twelve vegetable dishes. Snack on Hwangnaman bread on the street. Dinner *Ssanggye Sikdang* — ask for the abalone-braised fish, or **Omma Temple** if you want temple vegetarian (reserve the day before).

**Cafés on Hwangnidan-gil, all within a few minutes' walk:** **Kkachiseom** (꼬치섬) — second floor, straight view down onto the tomb mounds, ~US$4 · **Bitgguri** (빗꾸리) — century-old hanok teahouse, one-day tea classes on +82-54-777-4421 · **Eoseoseo** (어서어서) — independent bookshop, purchases wrapped in "읽는 약" envelopes · **NuMaru** — hanok café, daily 10:00–22:00.

> ⚠️ **Kkachiseom closes Tuesdays.** You are on a Friday, so it is open. Worth knowing anyway.

**Photo / film:** Bulguksa's stone terraces are 40 km away and tomorrow; tonight is Daereungwon's mounds in late light, then **Wolji Pond** with the pavilions reflected in still water — the single image this trip is built around. A fast prime beats a wide lens here.

**Rain backup:** Gyeongju National Museum — free, I. M. Pei building, close to Hwangnidan-gil. Then cafés and the covered part of Woljeongyo. **The pond does not need good weather**; it looks better on a calm wet night.

**Spend target:** US$64 rail · US$2 bus · US$8 transit · US$5 Wolji · US$60 food and tea.
