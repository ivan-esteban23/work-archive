# 29 October 2026 · DMZ and the Jeongdong night walk

[[Korea 2026 — Daily Pocket Cards|All days]] · [[Korea 2026 — Pocket 2026-10-28|← Previous]] · [[Korea 2026 — Pocket 2026-10-30|Next →]]

**Base:** back in Seoul · night 3 · **Hotel:** TBD · **Plan:** provisional

**Density:** 🟡 Full in the morning, deliberately empty in the afternoon. The 07:30 start is the one early morning of the trip.

**Main stop:** The DMZ — Imjingak, the Freedom Bridge, the 3rd Infiltration Tunnel, Dora Observatory.

**Route:** Coach pickup 07:30 (operator's message says where) → 08:30–12:30 the DMZ → drop-off City Hall ~13:30 → **metro to the hotel and actually rest** → Jeongdong Night Walk 19:00 → Insadong records.

[Imjingak Maps search](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=임진각+평화공원+파주) · [[Korea 2026 — Maps|Other stops]]

> ⚠️ **PASSPORT IN HAND.** Physical document, no copies, six months' validity. Checked at a military checkpoint before entering. Without it you do not go — and you will not get a refund.

> ⚠️ **Jeongdong Night Walk runs 29–31 October**, official opening evening on the **30th**. Tonight is lit but not the staged event. That is the trade for a weekday DMZ tour.

**Food / café:** Lunch at City Hall — *sajung* noodles from a basement *mulbaegu*, bibim-mul with a fried egg, no meat. Dinner *Eonnae Handaxe* (은애한식), Euljiro — banchan-led.

**Records:** Music Complex Seoul, Insadong-gil 49 — café and record store, **open till midnight**, 5 minutes from the drop-off.

**Books:** [Post Poetics](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Post+Poetics+bookstore+Hannam-dong+서울), Hannam-dong — English-language art books: photography, film, architecture, fashion, music. Worth the taxi.

**Photo / film:** Imjingak's reunification monuments; the tunnel's descent (no photography inside — helmets mandatory, steep and narrow); Dora's binocular view across the DMZ. Then the lit ginkgo wall at night.

**Rain backup:** The DMZ is unaffected — it is the one fixed item. If the walk is wet, substitute Cheonggyecheon, which is close to the drop-off and has covered stretches.

> 🔄 **If the tour is cancelled** (it does happen — military access is discretionary): Gyeongui Line to **Imjingang** at your own pace, vegetarian *sot bap* at **Baegyangsa Temple** (₩12,000, reserve a day ahead), then **Paju Book House** and **Odogae**. No tour, no passport needed. Better in some ways; you simply do not get the tunnel or the observatory.

**Spend target:** US$100 tour; US$55 food; US$20 records/books if you find something.
