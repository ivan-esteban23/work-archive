# 2 November 2026 · Busan to Gimhae, and home

[[Korea 2026 — Daily Pocket Cards|All days]] · [[Korea 2026 — Pocket 2026-11-01|← Previous]] · [[Korea 2026 — Start Here|Back to the dashboard]]

**Base:** Seomyeon · **Hotel:** TBD · **Plan:** confirmed flight required

**Density:** 🟢 Light. Nothing here needs effort and that is the point.

**Main stop:** Leave on time. The trip's only irreversible step.

**Route:** Slow breakfast → last coffee → check out and store bags → **Busan–Gimhae Light Rail from Sasang, ~50 min, ₩2,000** → Gimhae Airport by 11:30 for a 13:00 departure.

[Gimhae Airport Maps search](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=김해국제공항) · [[Korea 2026 — Maps|Other stops]]

**Food / café:** Breakfast near the hotel. Last coffee either on [Jeonpo Cafe Street](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=전포카페거리+부산) or at **Taikoo Li Gaegeum** — a sky-lit atrium, free, warm, and completely rain-proof if the morning is grey.

**Buy before boarding:** any last snacks, and check whether anything you bought needs a tax-refund receipt. Keep receipts for Global Tax Free purchases.

> ⚠️ **One ticket. Never self-transfer.** PUS→ICN→SCL, or PUS→NRT/HND→SCL. A self-transfer means re-checking bags and immigration, and a missed connection becomes a visa problem. The whole route was built to end at PUS so you would not have to do this at Incheon.

**Photo / film:** One last frame of Busan from the train if the weather is clear. Whatever you shot on Namsan, Inwangsan, Ikseon-dong, Wolji Pond or Gamcheon — back it up before you board, not after you land.

**Rain backup:** Not needed. Taikoo Li is indoors and the light rail is on rails.

**Spend target:** US$20 food · US$3 light rail (US$25 by taxi) · US$15–40 in purchases.
