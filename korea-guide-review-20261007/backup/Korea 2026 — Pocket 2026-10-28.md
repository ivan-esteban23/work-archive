# 28 October 2026 · Palaces, Jongno bookshops, Hongdae vintage

[[Korea 2026 — Daily Pocket Cards|All days]] · [[Korea 2026 — Pocket 2026-10-27|← Previous]] · [[Korea 2026 — Pocket 2026-10-29|Next →]]

**Base:** Jongno / Samcheong-dong · night 2 · **Hotel:** TBD · **Plan:** provisional

**Density:** 🟡 Full. Two clusters, 30 minutes apart. There is one real rest block at 15:30 — keep it.

**Main stop:** Gyeongbokgung at **09:30**, before the tour groups.

**Route:** Bus 29 or walk to Gyeongbokgung → Deoksugung stone wall path (8 min walk) → Samcheong-dong lunch → Boan Books and Dae-o Bookstore (Sajik-dong, 10 min) → **rest 15:30–17:30** → Line 3 to Line 2 for Hongdae (~30 min) → Hongdae vintage loop → walk to Yeonnam-dong.

[Gyeongbokgung Maps search](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=경복궁+서울) · [[Korea 2026 — Maps|Other stops]]

**Food / café:** Lunch *Sajae* (사재), Samcheong-dong — traditional set, vegetable-forward. Dinner Yeonnam-dong. **책익다**, Seochon, if you want a book and a whisky at the end.

**Shopping — the Hongdae loop,** all within a walk of Hongik University Exit 8/9: Page-One (closes 20:50) · ROOT HONGDAE (budget 2–3 h) · Hongdae Vintage B1 (open till 23:00) → then Yeonnam-dong micro-shops.

> ⚠️ **Mildboy is closed Wednesdays and Thursdays.** It is right beside ROOT and will look worth it. It is shut.

**Books:** **Boan Books** (보안서점) — a 1942 inn with a glass bridge and a palace-wall view, art and humanities, Tue–Sun 12–19. **Dae-o Bookstore** — 350 m away, Seoul's oldest bookshop, kept as a café, ₩7,000 entry with a drink.

**Photo / film:** Light through the Gyeongbokgung gates at 09:30, then the Deoksugung wall walkway, then Hongdae at dusk as the warehouses light up.

**Rain backup:** Both palaces have covered galleries. Cut the Hongdae loop entirely and keep Yeonnam-dong — same neighbourhood, no transit, calmer.

**Spend target:** US$85 food; US$5 bookshop entry; US$30 hanbok rental if you buy it; shopping uncapped.

- [ ] **Before leaving:** confirm the hanbok shop's location if you want the Gyeongbokgung entry waiver — wearing hanbok makes entry free.
- [ ] This morning: **check Naver Map for Page-One, ROOT and Hongdae B1** — confirm they are open today.
- [ ] Phone/camera charged; layers for the evening; ₩20,000 note.
