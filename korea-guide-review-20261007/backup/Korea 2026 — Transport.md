## 🚄 The two rail legs

| Leg | Route | Duration | Fare (standard) | Total for two |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **30 Oct, morning** | Seoul Station → Gyeongju | 1h58m – 2h15m | ₩44,500 pp | **US$64** |
| **31 Oct, afternoon** | Gyeongju → Busan | 28–36 min | ₩10,100 pp | **US$15** |

**Departs from Seoul Station (서울역).** 23 weekday services from 05:13 to 21:33.

> [!danger] The Gyeongju station-name question, resolved
> Older guides say the KTX stops at **Singyeongju (신경주역)** and that Gyeongju Station is a slower, regional-only stop. Current data says otherwise: both Wikidata and OpenStreetMap place the high-speed stop at **경주역, 35.798, 129.139**, and both treat 경주역 and 신경주역 as the same location. **That confusion comes from older material, not from your booking.** Either way the town is ~12 km from the platform:
> - **Bus 50, 51 or 70** — ₩1,500, about 30 minutes, from roughly 05:00 to 23:00
> - **Taxi** — ₩15,000, about 10 minutes, worth it on a transfer day
>
> Check the station name printed on your ticket when it arrives, and allow the transfer in your morning either way.

> [!tip] Booking early buys a seat, not a discount
> Korail fares are **regulated and fixed** — ₩44,500 is the same price a month out or an hour before departure. What changes is availability. Friday evenings, Sunday afternoons and holidays fill; a weekday usually does not. Book the outbound and return together.

### Why not the KORAIL Pass?

**Because on this route it costs more.** The pass is ₩131,000 for two days (~US$95) for the cheapest tier. Your exact routing on separate tickets is **₩109,200 (~US$79)** — about **US$16 cheaper**.

The pass only pays off when a single travel day carries **two long rides**, or when you are covering a wider multi-city route. You have one long ride per day. If you add a day-trip, recalculate before dismissing it.

### Booking

| What | Where | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single tickets | **korail.com** or the Korail+ app | Needs passport and card. Seats open about a month ahead |
| Pass, if you reconsider | korail.com | Name must match your passport; **not refundable** once the start date arrives |
| Last resort | Station counters | Seats are assigned; a ticket without a seat number is a standing ticket |

| | Incheon (ICN) | Gimhae (PUS) |

| Arrives / departs | 27 Oct | 2 Nov |
| City | ~50 km from central Seoul | ~15 km from Seomyeon |
| **City link** | **AREX express, 43 min to Seoul Station, ₩9,500 pp** | **Busan–Gimhae Light Rail**, ~50 min from Sasang, ₩2,000 |
| Backup | Taxi, ~US$80+ | Taxi ₩28,000–35,000 (~US$22) |
| Budget line | US$14 for two | US$3 for two, +US$22 by taxi |

> [!danger] Gimhae is a small airport — go early
> PUS international is compact, but roadworks around Gwangalli have slowed traffic. Leave Seomyeon by 11:30 for a 13:00 flight. The light rail takes the traffic out of the equation entirely; the taxi does not.

> [!tip] Why the trip ends in Busan and not Seoul
> Flying out of PUS rather than returning to ICN removes a 50 km transfer and, more importantly, lets the whole route run south without backtracking. It also opens direct connections to the Americas through Seoul or Tokyo **on one ticket**. See [[Korea 2026 — Practical Info|the booking warning]] on self-transfers.

## 🚌 The DMZ day — 29 October

| Detail | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Pickup | **07:30–08:00**, usually Jongno or City Hall — operator-dependent |
| Return | About 13:30 |
| Distance | The DMZ is 50–60 km north of central Seoul; 1–1.5 hours by coach |
| Cost | **US$42–60 pp** including transport, guide and admissions to the 3rd Tunnel and Dora Observatory |
| Booking | **Passport details required at booking.** Physical passport checked on the day — copies refused |
| JSA | **Not available to ordinary tourists.** No operator is selling it to tourists as of mid-2026. Military-affiliated access only |

**What is included:** Imjingak Peace Park, the Freedom Bridge, the 3rd Infiltration Tunnel and its exhibition hall, and Dora Observatory with binoculars. Shuttle-only tickets run US$25–35 but exclude the tunnel and observatory entries and the guide — that is a different product.

**Photography:** prohibited inside the tunnel. Helmets are mandatory and it is steep, narrow and stepped — 265 m long, 1.95 m high, 2 m wide.

> [!tip] If the tour is cancelled
> Military access is discretionary and cancellations do happen. The swap needs **no tour, no booking and no passport**: Gyeongui Line to **Imjingang** (60–75 minutes), vegetarian *sot bap* at **Baegyangsa Temple**, then **Paju Book House** and **Odogae**. Quieter and more rewarding in some ways — you simply do not get the tunnel or the observatory, which is the whole trade.

## 🚇 City transit

**T-money** works on Seoul metro, buses, and Busan's system, and on the AREX and light rail with a small top-up. Buy at any convenience store or airport kiosk.

| | Base fares | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seoul metro / bus | ~₩1,400 / ₩1,200 | Minimum ~₩5,800 per trip, capped around ₩9,000 |
| Busan | Similar | |
| Bus 50/51/70, Gyeongju | ₩1,500 | ₩1,450 with a card |

**Budget line: US$95** covering T-money top-ups in all three cities plus the airport transfers.

> [!tip] One card per person, and keep it topped up
> A physical T-money card behaves like a transit card in both cities, and gets you the ₩50 bus discount. Do not rely on a phone-based card — it works inconsistently across operators and models.
