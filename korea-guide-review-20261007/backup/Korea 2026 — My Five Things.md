## 🍂 1. The Wolji Pond reflection

**Slot:** Day 4, 20:30–22:00 · [[Korea 2026 — Pocket 2026-10-30|30 October]]

Silla pavilions reflected in still water, lit from below, on a cold October night. ₩3,000, last entry 21:30.

**Why this one:** it is the image the trip is built around, and it is the reason you are sleeping in Gyeongju rather than day-tripping from Seoul. A same-day visit cannot do this without watching the clock.

**Protect it by:** getting the KTX booked so Friday is unhurried, booking Gyeongju lodging **inside Hwangnidan-gil** so there is no taxi back, and packing a tripod or finding a stable surface. Fast prime, not wide. See [[Korea 2026 — Photography and Filming#Day 4 · Fri 30 Oct — Gyeongju, and the anchor image|the shot plan]].

## 🎧 2. An hour in a listening room

**Slot:** flexible — Day 1 evening or Day 3 · [[Korea 2026 — Cafés and Shops|Wanita, Seongsu]]

A dedicated room, vintage speakers, a player who puts on what you ask for. ₩16,500 including a drink, about US$24 for two.

**Why this one:** you said you wanted records, and Korea does this better than almost anywhere. It is also the most restful hour available in a city that is otherwise six days of walking. You cannot rush a listening room, which is exactly why it belongs on this list.

**Protect it by:** giving it a real hour rather than a browse. It is **not currently scheduled** — the honest version of this item means deliberately building it in, or accepting that it happens only if energy allows.

## 👖 3. One piece of Korean vintage

**Slot:** Day 2, 18:00–20:00 · Hongdae loop · [[Korea 2026 — Cafés and Shops#👕 Vintage and thrift|the shop list]]

## 🧱 4. Walking into the Third Infiltration Tunnel

**Slot:** Day 3, 08:30–12:30 · [[Korea 2026 — Pocket 2026-10-29|29 October]]

73 metres underground, 265 metres long, cut north from North Korea in 1978, ending at a barricade. Steep, stepped, narrow, helmets mandatory. **Photography is prohibited inside** — which is part of why it counts.

**Why this one:** it is the most physically affecting historical thing in the trip, and it is not a monument or a museum. It is a hole somebody dug. You will not forget the walk down.

**Protect it by:** booking the tour, carrying the passport, and wearing the gloves. The 07:30 coach is the early start you accepted; this is what it buys.

> 🔄 **If the tour is cancelled:** the swap — Gyeongui Line to Imjingang, vegetarian *sot bap* at Baegyangsa Temple, Paju Book House and Odogae — gives you Baegyangsa and Paju but **not** the tunnel. This one cannot be replaced. If the tunnel matters more than the plan, the honest response is to run the swap and accept a lesser day, or to reschedule.

## 🍲 5. One meal worth remembering

**Slot:** flexible · [[Korea 2026 — Food|Food and vegetarian options]]

Your call in advance so you are actually paying attention to it. The candidates:

| | Where | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ssanggye Sikdang** | Gyeongju | The legendary local room. Order *maeuntak*, or **hobachui** — abalone braised in soy and sake |
| **Omma Temple** | Gyeongju | Buddhist temple cooking, ₩12,000. The most interesting vegetarian meal in Korea, and a temple rather than a restaurant |
| **Hwangnam Gukbap** | Gyeongju | *Sarangchae* — nine to twelve small vegetable dishes. The defining Gyeongju meal |
| **Sisters Naejangsan** | Busan | Michelin-listed, excellent banchan, easy without meat |
| **Makdong Toseong** | Seoul | Halal, gukbap, the vegetable pot is a real dish |

**Why this one:** food is the reason you listed it first among your interests, and it is the thing a photograph cannot do for you.

**Protect it by:** picking it in advance, booking where booking is needed (**Omma Temple reserves a day ahead**), and not eating standing up.

## 🧭 When two plans compete

> [!danger] These five win. Everything else moves.
> If Day 2 runs long and you must choose between the Hongdae vintage loop and finishing the Deoksugung wall at dusk in good light — **the wall wins**, and item 3 becomes a Seongsu find instead. If Day 6 has four blocks and you can only manage three, **Haedong Yonggungsa goes** and Gwangalli sunset stays, because the sunset is item 1's cousin and Haedong has its own card in [[Korea 2026 — Photography and Filming|Photography]].
>
> **Nothing on this list may be sacrificed to make room for something else.** The five are the trip; the anchors are the plan's way of delivering them.

### The things that are *not* on this list

And that is deliberate: the DMZ observatory telescope view, the Seoul Forest ginkgo grove, the guard-change ceremony, the fall-leaf carpet at Deoksugung, most of the vintage circuit, the Yongdusan cable car, most of the cafés.

They are all worth doing. **None of them is why you came.**
