# 27 October 2026 · Arrival and Ikseon-dong

[[Korea 2026 — Daily Pocket Cards|All days]] · [[Korea 2026 — Pocket 2026-10-28|Next →]]

**Base:** Euljiro / Ikseon-dong · night 1 · **Hotel:** TBD · **Plan:** provisional

**Density:** 🟢 Light. You have been in the air ~24 hours. Do not schedule anything needing concentration.

**Main stop:** Land, drop bags, and let Ikseon-dong do the work.

**Route:** ICN T1 → immigration → AREX express to Seoul Station (43 min, ₩9,500) → store bags (check-in 15:00) → Euljiro. Walk everywhere after that.

[Ikseon-dong Maps search](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=익선동+서울) · [[Korea 2026 — Maps|Other stops]]

**Food / café:** Late brunch at *Makdong Toseong* (gukbap, ask for the vegetable pot). Café at *Dalkom*, Ikseon-dong — walnut cake, go early. Dinner at Gwangjang Market: bindaetteok, tteokbokki.

**Optional:** Gwangjang Market 2F vintage floor. Follow the red 수입구제 signs upstairs — fifty to eighty indie stalls and almost no tourists. You are already there for dinner.

**Records:** The DISK, Sogong-dong underground — used vinyl, cheap, daily 13:00–20:00. Only if you still have the energy.

**Photo / film:** Ikseon-dong's 1930s hanok blocks in flat afternoon light, then the Gyeongui Line forest walkway looking down on the tracks. Bring the wide lens for the alleys.

**Rain backup:** Stay in Seomyeon — coffee, shopping, no walking required. Skip Ikseon entirely.

**Spend target:** US$70 food and drink; US$14 airport; shopping is uncapped today, set your figure in [[Korea 2026 — Budget|Budget]].
