# 31 October 2026 · Bulguksa, then south to Busan

[[Korea 2026 — Daily Pocket Cards|All days]] · [[Korea 2026 — Pocket 2026-10-30|← Previous]] · [[Korea 2026 — Pocket 2026-11-01|Next →]]

**Base:** Seomyeon / Gwangbok-dong · night 5 · **Hotel:** TBD · **Plan:** provisional

**Density:** 🟡 Full. Second transfer day — pack in the morning, do not check out late.

**Main stop:** Bulguksa at **08:30**, before the tour buses.

**Route:** Bus **700** from Gyeongju (~20 min) → Bulguksa 08:30–10:15 → Seokguram *optional* 10:30–11:45 → bus back, lunch → **KTX 14:20, 28–36 min, ₩10,100** → Busan Station 14:55 → Seomyeon, check in → Yongdusan Cable Car 16:30 for golden hour → Gwangbok-dong → dinner → Jeonpo.

[Bulguksa Maps search](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=불국사+경주) · [[Korea 2026 — Maps|Other stops]]

**Food / café:** Lunch *Hwangnam Gukbap* again, or *Ssanggye* if you held out yesterday. Dinner *Jejuga*, Gwangbok-dong — traditional Busan set, very vegetable-forward.

> 🎃 **It is Halloween — and you are in the right place.**
> Halloween 2026 falls today, the first Saturday-on-Halloween since 2022, and **Seoul runs it as a public-safety operation** — ~110,000 people were in Hongdae by 22:00 last year. **You are in Busan**, which has no equivalent street party. Your evening is Gwangbok-dong arcades, a cable car and dinner. Nothing to avoid, nothing to plan around.
>
> ⚠️ One rule if you hear about Seoul: **dressing as a police officer is a criminal offence in Korea** — up to six months or a ₩3M fine. Full context in [[Korea 2026 — Practical Info#🎃 Halloween weekend, 27 Oct – 1 Nov|Halloween weekend]].

**Records, if it is open:** **Vintage 38**, Jeonpo — **open 24 hours**, an industrial café with a wall of vinyl and vintage electronics. Works whenever you actually land, which makes it the reliable late option. **Knockout** (넉아웃), Bujeon-dong — vintage café by day, bar by night, rooftop.

**Photo / film:** Bulguksa's Dabotap and Seokgatap pagodas with the mountain still empty behind them. From the Yongdusan cable car: the harbour, the container port and Haeundae Bridge in low sun.

**Rain backup:** Skip Seokguram — it is the most weather-exposed block on this trip, a twenty-minute uphill bus on top of an already full morning. Bulguksa's covered halls are enough. In Busan, Gwangbok-dong's arcades and the cable car both work wet.

**Spend target:** US$15 rail · US$18 admission · US$18 cable car · US$55 food · US$20 local transit.
