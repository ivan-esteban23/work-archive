> [!warning] Record the cancellation time zone, not just the date
> Korean providers usually quote in KST (UTC+9). Chile is UTC−4 in October, and Santiago will be on UTC−3 by early November when you land back — so the date can shift under you. Write the cutoff as a date **and** a time **and** a zone. Getting this wrong costs a night's lodging.

## 🔄 The DMZ, and its swap

> [!tip] Book the guided tour first, hold the swap as plan B
> The swap — Gyeongui Line to Imjingang, vegetarian lunch at Baegyangsa Temple, Paju Book House and Odogae — needs no tour, no booking and no passport. It is not a consolation prize; it is genuinely quieter. What it gives up is the Third Tunnel and Dora Observatory, which cannot be reached on your own.

**If the DMZ tour is cancelled** (military access is discretionary and it does happen):

- [ ] Check the operator's message by 06:00 and decide before you leave the hotel
- [ ] Call Baegyangsa Temple a day ahead if you can, otherwise go early and expect to take what is ready
- [ ] Take the Gyeongui Line to Imjingang at your own pace — 60–75 minutes from central Seoul
- [ ] Finish at Paju Book House and Odogae, then return for the Jeongdong night walk
