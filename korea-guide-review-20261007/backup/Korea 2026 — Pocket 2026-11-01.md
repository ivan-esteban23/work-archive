# 1 November 2026 · Jagalchi, Gamcheon, Millak-dong, the coast

[[Korea 2026 — Daily Pocket Cards|All days]] · [[Korea 2026 — Pocket 2026-10-31|← Previous]] · [[Korea 2026 — Pocket 2026-11-02|Next →]]

**Base:** Seomyeon · night 6 · **Hotel:** TBD · **Plan:** provisional

**Density:** 🟠 The densest day. Five short blocks, all an hour or two. **The cut is marked — take it if you need to.**

**Main stop:** Gamcheon at **09:45**, before the crowds.

**Route:** Jagalchi Market 08:30 → taxi to **Gamcheon** 09:45–11:15 → lunch in Seo-gu → taxi to **Millak-dong** 13:00–16:00 → **Haedong Yonggungsa** 16:30–18:00 *(the cut)* → Gwangalli sunset and dinner.

[Jagalchi Maps search](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=자갈치시장+부산) · [[Korea 2026 — Maps|Other stops]]

> ⚠️ **Gamcheon at 09:45, not at 14:00.** The lanes are ~3.5 m wide with 300+ steps. At 09:45 you get the light and the quiet; after lunch it is shoulder to shoulder. Wear the good shoes — this is the one place that will hurt if you came in new trainers.

**Food / café:** Breakfast at Jagalchi — bindaetteok and hotteok, cooked in front of you. Lunch *Sisters Naejangsan*, Seo-gu — Michelin-listed, excellent banchan, ask for the vegetable set. Dinner Gwangalli or Haeundae Market.

**🎨 Free and unplanned: Busan Biennale 2026 ends today.** It has been running since 29 August at the **Busan Museum of Contemporary Art**, in the former Busan Nam High School, plus Space OneZ. **Your last full day in the country is its final day.** Check opening hours on the museum site in the morning — if it closes early, it does not replace Haedong, it replaces the Millak-dong café block. [Busan Museum of Contemporary Art](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=부산현대미술관)

**Millak-dong cluster, 13:00–16:00:**
- **Cafe Voisin** — your landmark: pointed roof, floor-to-ceiling glass, "we serve coffee" over a bath pictogram, beside the old Okcheontang public bath
- **Dopamine Records** (도파민레코드) — record bar in the middle of the street
- **Casual Book Shop** — bookshop-café in a retro apricot-tiled building
- **MILLAC THE MARKET** — daily **10:00–24:00**, busking square

> 💡 **MILLAC is the fallback.** Individual alley cafés each set their own hours and the city guide only says "varies". If three are shut, go to MILLAC.

**Records / books elsewhere:** **Dambug** (담벅), Haeundae — bookshop. **Waveon Coffee**, Gwangalli — a 1920s Japanese-style villa, the best building in Busan.

**Photo / film:** Gamcheon's lanes in morning light. Haedong Yonggungsa's thirteenth-century sea-cliff temple with waves through the courtyard. Gwangalli at sunset over the bridge.

**Rain backup:** Drop Gamcheon **and** Haedong. Go to MILLAC, the market floor, and Jeonpo — all covered, and it is a better shopping day in the wet anyway.

**Spend target:** US$35 food · US$35 cafés and records · US$25 transit (two taxis) · shopping uncapped, ship it if it will not fit.
