<callout icon="🦇" color="purple_bg">
	**Start with your mood**
	**Raw deathrock:** Bat-Cave Productions. **Guitar-led goth/post-punk:** Manic Depression and SwissDarkNights. **Synth/coldwave exploration:** Cold Transmission, Fabrika and Wave Records. **Atmospheric detour:** Projekt. These are browsing suggestions, not a claim that every record in each catalog matches your taste.

<summary>Link checks and using label catalogs</summary>
	The Bandcamp links are the labels’ catalog or artist-directory pages, rather than listener collections. Country/base and catalog clues come from those pages. Official domains were cross-checked against the labels’ own links where available; SwissDarkNights also has its named official site.
	AVANT!, Danse Macabre, Fabrika and InClub link to the listed domains from their Bandcamp pages, but their website pages could not be read by the research browser. Their website links are retained as official destinations; current website contents and shop availability were not verified. SwissDarkNights has an image-led website with limited readable text.
	Label shops may also sell other labels’ releases. A shop listing does not by itself prove a record is on that label. Check the release credits and catalog number, distinguish original releases from reissues, and compare the exact vinyl/CD/cassette edition before buying.
	For discovery, choose one label and one sampler or artist directory, save two promising album URLs, then add your listening reaction to the journal. The directory is a starting selection, not a complete census of worldwide labels.
</details>
