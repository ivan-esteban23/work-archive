# The Visual Edit — Country Audit 2026-10-13

Country/base records the source operator, posted editorial base, project team or linked national edition. It does not describe owner nationality, the countries of individual contributors, or everywhere a tool can be used. Coverage retains the directory’s existing research-focus grouping and is not an exhaustive geography of published content.

**50 sources have country labels across 16 countries.** Shaarli and r/femalefashionadvice are explicitly classified as an international project and an international community. Their hosting providers’ countries are not substituted for a publisher location.

Country evidence was checked on 2026-10-13. Existing source-review dates and access labels are preserved; this country audit does not claim fresh verification of subscriptions or logged-in features. Official contact/legal/about pages are preferred. Company-authored GitHub, app-store and LinkedIn profiles are identified in the notes; Vestoj uses an industry editorial biography corroborated by a university-hosted event biography.
