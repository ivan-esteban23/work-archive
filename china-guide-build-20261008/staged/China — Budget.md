## Build the estimate

| Category | Calculation to record |
| --- | --- |
| Hotels | Sum full-stay totals for each base, including taxes and fees |
| Food and cafés | Personal daily allowance × local trip days × travelers |
| Intercity transport | Each actual rail/flight fare plus baggage and city transfers |
| Local transport | Airport/station transfers plus metro/taxis |
| Attractions and tours | Ticket or package amount × applicable travelers, including chosen extras |
| Connectivity / insurance / entry | Actual provider totals and applicable visa costs |
| Shopping / gifts | Personal cap; keep separate from reserve |
| Reserve | Unallocated contingency |
| International travel if included | Complete flights plus San Fernando–SCL ground travel |

**Total = all included categories + reserve.** Record exchange-rate date, conversion source, and card fees. A hotel room price is not automatically a per-person amount; a tour’s “from” price is not automatically a group total.
