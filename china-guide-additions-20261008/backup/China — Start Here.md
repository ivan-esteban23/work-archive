## First route to compare

**Beijing 5 nights → Xi’an 3 nights → Shanghai 6 nights = 14 nights / 15 local calendar days.** This is a suggested starting scenario, not a selected itinerary. International travel from Chile adds travel time outside those 15 days.

Start with imperial Beijing and a Great Wall outing, continue to the Terracotta Warriors and Xi’an food, then slow down for Shanghai photography, cafés, and browsing. Protect ordinary neighborhood time alongside the big sights.

| Direction | Route sketch | Main tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| History, cities, and photography | Beijing 5 + Xi’an 3 + Shanghai 6 | My starting recommendation; two internal transfer days |
| Food and a slower middle chapter | Beijing 4 + Chengdu 4 + Shanghai 6 | Replaces Xi’an; Chengdu places and transport need research |
| Landscapes as the main priority | Beijing 4 + Zhangjiajie 4 + Shanghai 6 | Candidate for further research; weather, park access, and connections determine feasibility |
| Shorter first visit | Beijing 5 + Shanghai 5 | 10 nights, fewer transfers; no Xi’an |

All night counts are examples. Route direction should follow the best complete flight itinerary, not force a costly return to the first city.
