## Biography

Fukuda is represented by **Bambi Promotion**, whose official roster lists her. Her public profile identifies **December 2, 2025** as her MOODYZ debut. [Agency roster](https://bambi.ne.jp/?id=345) · [Public profile](https://1link.jp/yua_fucuda)

Before adopting her current name, she worked in gravure under **Mashiro Asagiri**, according to secondary biographies. The precise starting year of that earlier career remains insufficiently verified for this note. [Moviefone](https://www.moviefone.com/celebrity/yua-fukuda/VZ5zRmt11FA2PDxAgpkvQ3/main/)

### The story behind her name

She says a producer associated her frequent smile with good fortune, inspiring the name **Fukuda**. The character **福** means good fortune or happiness. Her profile invites fans to use **Yua-chan** or **Yuachi**, and lists **strawberries, anime, and solo travel** among her interests. [Her own profile](https://1link.jp/yua_fucuda)
