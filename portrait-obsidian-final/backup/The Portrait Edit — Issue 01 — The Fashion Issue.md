### Photographer’s notes

**Lens language:** portrait compression and shallow backgrounds for intimate studies, standard perspective for full-length fashion.

**Lighting:** use one clearly defined lighting approach per image. Amber direct light for street scenes, soft high key diffusion for studio studies, directional cinema light for the gothic scenes.

**Composition:** protect hair silhouettes, jewelry and garment details. Leave negative space where editorial text might be added later.

**Continuity for new prompt generation:** all generated subjects should be original adults and should not reproduce the identities of photographed people.
