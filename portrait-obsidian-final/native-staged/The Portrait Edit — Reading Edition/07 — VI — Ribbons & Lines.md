### 14 · Monochrome Line
*Full-body minimalist lookbook: black ribbed turtleneck, · PDF page 14*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-14-selected.png|850]]

white mini skirt, white knee socks and pointed black shoes. The white background keeps attention on silhouette and contrast.

**Art direction**
Clean black-and-white styling, crisp studio light and full-length framing.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Full-length vertical fashion lookbook photograph of an original adult woman standing straight and facing the camera, long straight glossy black hair with a center part falling past the shoulders, calm neutral expression and direct gaze. She wears a fitted black ribbed-knit turtleneck with long sleeves, a clean white A-line mini skirt, white ribbed knee-high socks and pointed black patent flats. Arms relaxed at her sides, slightly elongated model pose, pure white seamless studio background, soft even frontal lighting with minimal shadows, crisp fabric and rib-knit texture, strict black and white palette with natural skin tones, minimalist contemporary e-commerce editorial style, sharp focus from head to toe, no text, logos or props.
