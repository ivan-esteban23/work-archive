### 07 · Ivory Knit
*Romantic Minimalism · PDF page 10*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-07-selected.png|850]]

Copper red hair pairs with a cream coordinated cardigan and miniskirt, neat knit textures, a short chain necklace and a dark handbag.

### 08 · Powder Blue
*Playful Texture · PDF page 11*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-08-selected.png|850]]

A fitted pale blue cardigan and pleated skirt contrast with white fluffy leg warmers and black loafers. Oversized hoops and a small beaded bag give the outfit an early 2000s influence.

**Art direction**
Full-body framing, pale blue and white, dark accessory accents, clear textures.

### 09 · Ochre Study
*Retro Form · PDF page 11*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-09-selected.png|850]]

The ochre fitted dress uses a Mandarin-inspired collar, an oval neckline detail, side gathering and a twisted bodice. Long copper hair adds tonal contrast.

**Art direction**
Neutral studio lighting and no distracting props. Keep the dress silhouette legible.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Full length studio fashion editorial of an original adult woman with long copper-red wavy hair wearing a fitted ochre mustard mini dress, long sleeves, Mandarin inspired high collar, small oval neckline cutout, crossed twist detail at the bodice, gently ruched sides. Minimal silver rings and small blush handbag, pure white seamless background, soft even studio lighting, 1970s inspired modern fashion styling, clear garment detail, no lettering.
