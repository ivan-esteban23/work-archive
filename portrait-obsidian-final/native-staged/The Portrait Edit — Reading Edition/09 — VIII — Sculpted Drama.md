### 23 · Slate Couture
*Full-length look in a bright minimalist lobby: a sleeveless · PDF page 20*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-23-selected.png|850]]

black beaded-lace mock-neck bodice, a high-waisted charcoal wool circle skirt with sculpted seams, glittering black lace-up boots and long wavy black hair. Floor-to-ceiling windows, cream bouclé seating and a swirled rose-and-gray carpet complete the setting.

**Art direction**
Modern haute couture silhouette, charcoal and black palette with soft pink accents, luxurious interior, gentle smile.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic vertical full-length fashion photograph of an original adult East Asian woman with long, glossy wavy black hair, smiling softly at the camera. She wears a sleeveless black beaded-lace mock-neck bodice with a diamond pattern, a dramatic high-waisted charcoal gray wool circle skirt with sculpted vertical seams that falls to mid-calf, and glittering black sequin lace-up ankle boots. She stands in a bright luxury boutique lobby with floor-to-ceiling windows, cream and blush bouclé armchairs, a polished marble floor, a blank pale wood reception wall with no signage, and a circular swirled rose-and-gray carpet. Natural daylight, soft warm ceiling light strip, clean architectural perspective, high-end couture editorial, sharp detail on wool texture and beadwork, no text or logos.
