### 66 · Bow Tartan
*Heritage Tartan · PDF page 48*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-66-selected.png|850]]

A brown tartan midi dress printed with small black bows, with a square neckline gathered by a lace-up front, ruffled edge and a fishtail hem. Black velvet bows in the hair and a velvet choker complete the look against a pale studio backdrop.

**Art direction**
Heritage tartan and velvet bows, warm brown against black, a calm full-length frame with room around the silhouette.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic full-length vertical fashion lookbook photograph of an original adult woman with long light-brown hair in loose waves, pulled back with two black velvet bow clips, a calm direct gaze and natural makeup. She wears a brown tartan midi dress printed with small black bow motifs, a square neckline gathered by a lace-up front with a ruffled edge, fitted long sleeves and a fishtail hem. A black velvet choker with a small pendant, a dark brown leather crossbody bag and black velvet mules. Pale seamless studio backdrop, soft even diffused light, rich brown and black palette, crisp fabric and velvet texture, contemporary heritage fashion campaign, no text or logos.

### 69 · Scarlet Column

### 71 · Gold Laurel
*Gilded Crown · PDF page 51*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-71-selected.png|850]]

A runway close-up of long honey-brown hair parted at the center, beneath a gold leaf crown. Smoky, graphic eyeliner and soft peach lips sit above a sequined bodice in silver tulle lace, against a warm blurred background.

**Art direction**
Antique gold and silver against warm honey tones, dramatic eye makeup, a soft glow in the background.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic vertical runway close-up portrait of an original adult woman with very long, honey-brown hair parted in the center and falling in soft waves, a gold leaf crown across the top of her head, pale blue-gray eyes with dramatic smoky graphic eyeliner, defined brows and soft peach lips. She wears a sheer silver tulle neckline with sequined and beaded lace over a bodice, and small gold drop earrings. Warm, softly blurred background in amber and deep red, soft frontal runway light, shallow depth of field, antique gold and silver palette, regal couture mood, sharp detail on the crown, no text or logos.
