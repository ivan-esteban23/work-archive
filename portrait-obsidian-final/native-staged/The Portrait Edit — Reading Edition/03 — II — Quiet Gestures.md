### 02 · Quiet Beauty
*High Key · PDF page 5*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-02-selected.png|850]]

A minimalist beauty portrait with glossy black hair, loose strands crossing the face, hands draped over a bare shoulder and a silver ring. Pale blue gray tones convey restraint.

**Art direction**
Luminous white background, delicate hair movement, soft shadow boundaries, precise hand posing.
