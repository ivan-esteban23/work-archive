### 78 · Ivory Column
*Long Line · PDF page 56*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-78-selected.png|850]]

A long-sleeved ivory turtleneck and a fluid, asymmetric ivory midi skirt with a sheer hem, worn with a dark shoulder bag and tall brown buckled boots. Long light-brown waves and a pale cream sock peek out against a textured beige wall and a blue carpet stacked with books.

**Art direction**
Ivory and cream against warm sand and blue carpet, a grounded full-length pose and soft, even daylight.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic full-length vertical fashion editorial of an original adult woman with long, straight light-brown hair falling past her shoulders, a calm, direct gaze and soft natural makeup with a slightly parted lip. She wears a fitted ivory long-sleeve turtleneck and a flowing asymmetric ivory midi skirt with a sheer, uneven hem, a cream ribbed sock visible at one ankle, a dark leather crossbody bag with a wide strap, and tall brown distressed leather knee boots with buckled straps. Standing on a plush cobalt-blue carpet, with stacks of books on either side, in front of a textured warm sand-colored plaster wall, soft directional daylight, ivory, sand and cobalt palette, crisp knit texture, contemporary autumn fashion campaign, no text or logos.

### 79 · Ribbed Cami
*Street Minimal · PDF page 57*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-79-selected.png|850]]

A fitted black ribbed camisole dress with thin straps, worn with a long slit skirt. Long, loose waves fall past the shoulders as the model sits with her arms resting on her knees, beside a large dark leather bag.

**Art direction**
Black against pale grey-green walls, soft, slightly high-key light and an intimate, relaxed seated pose.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic vertical seated editorial portrait of an original adult woman with very long, loosely waved dark brown hair falling over her shoulders, soft bangs, a calm, slightly downcast gaze and soft red lips. She wears a fitted black ribbed camisole dress with thin straps, with a long side-slit black skirt. Her hands rest together on one knee, and a large dark grey leather bag fills the right edge of the frame. Pale grey-green plaster wall with a low beige paneled base, soft even window light, black and neutral palette, clean fabric rib texture, minimal street-style editorial, no text or logos.

### 80 · Denim Mini
*Off Duty · PDF page 57*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-80-selected.png|850]]

A seated, relaxed pose in a cream ribbed turtleneck, a light-wash denim mini skirt and knee-high black suede boots. Long, straight dark hair falls to the waist, with arms crossed as the model sits on a deep blue sofa in a bright retail interior.

**Art direction**
Cream and light denim against deep blue upholstery, bright showroom light and a casual, poised stance.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic full-length vertical lifestyle portrait of an original adult woman seated on the edge of a deep blue upholstered bench, long straight dark hair with soft wispy bangs, a calm, composed gaze and soft red lips, arms folded across her chest. She wears a cream ribbed fitted turtleneck, a light-wash denim mini skirt with a button front, and knee-high black suede boots with flat soles, with small silver hoop earrings. Bright retail interior with a pale wooden floor, a red clothing rail with quilted jackets and denim pieces, and a dark wall with folded knitwear on shelves, soft even store lighting, cream, denim and navy palette, clean contemporary fashion editorial, no text or logos.

### 81 · Camel Tailoring
*Brown Suiting · PDF page 58*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-81-selected.png|850]]

A sharply cut camel-brown blazer with peaked lapels, worn open over a matching pair of tailored shorts, a striped button-up shirt and a fitted ribbed cardigan in a soft pink. Long, glossy black hair falls around the shoulders, and oversized glasses frame a steady gaze in front of a soft grey studio backdrop.

**Art direction**
Warm camel and blush against a soft grey studio wall, a directional light stripe across the background and a strong, poised silhouette.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic vertical full-length studio fashion portrait of an original adult woman with long, glossy black wavy hair parted in the center, a calm, steady gaze and soft rose blush with glossy rose lips, standing with her weight on one leg. She wears a camel-brown wool-look blazer with peaked lapels, worn open over a fitted ribbed blush cardigan and a crisp striped cream button-up shirt collar, a matching camel tailored short with a high waist, a slim silver rimless pair of glasses, and a thin beaded necklace. Soft grey gradient studio backdrop with a diagonal soft light beam across the wall and a light concrete floor, warm camel and blush palette, crisp tailoring, fine fabric texture, polished autumn fashion editorial, no text or logos.

### 82 · Pleat Sport
*Sporty Preppy · PDF page 58*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-82-selected.png|850]]

A sporty look in a white long-sleeve zip-neck top with a navy collar, a pale sky-blue pleated mini skirt and bright blue ribbed knee socks. Chunky white loafers, a white padded headband and a green practice basket with golf club complete the outfit against a pale railing and a beige wall.

**Art direction**
Sky blue, navy and white against a warm beige wall and grey railing, bright daylight, a full-length, upright stance.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic full-length vertical fashion lookbook photograph of an original adult woman with long, straight dark hair parted in the center and a white padded headband, a composed, neutral expression with soft red lips, standing upright and facing the camera. She wears a white long-sleeve pullover with a navy zip-up collar, a pale sky-blue pleated mini skirt, bright cobalt-blue ribbed knee-high leg warmers and white chunky platform loafers with gold buckles. She holds a bright green wire practice basket of golf balls in one hand, with a black-handled golf club resting against the railing beside her. Pale beige wall, grey metal railing, soft even daylight, white, navy and sky-blue palette, crisp knit and pleat texture, sporty preppy campaign, no text or logos.

### 83 · Bow Ruffle
*Collared Navy · PDF page 59*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-83-selected.png|850]]

A navy textured long-sleeve mini dress with a pale blue Peter Pan collar and cuffs, a bodice of stacked ruffles tied with a dark self-tie bow and a ruffled hem. Dark knee-high socks, brown chunky loafers and a brown leather shoulder bag complete the look against a soft grey studio backdrop.

**Art direction**
Navy and pale blue on a soft grey backdrop, collegiate romance, clean full-length framing and even studio light.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic full-length vertical fashion lookbook photograph of an original adult woman with long, light-brown hair falling past her shoulders, silver hoop earrings, pale blue-grey eyes and a calm, neutral gaze. She wears a navy textured long-sleeve mini dress with a pale blue Peter Pan collar and cuffs, a bodice of vertical ruffled panels tied at the center with a dark self-tie bow, a fitted waist and a ruffled wave hem. Dark knee-high socks with a lighter grey cuff, chunky brown leather loafers with squared toes, and a slim brown leather shoulder bag with gold hardware. Pale grey seamless studio background, soft even light, navy and pale blue palette, collegiate romantic styling, no text or logos.
