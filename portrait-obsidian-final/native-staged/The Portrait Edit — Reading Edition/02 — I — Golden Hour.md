### 01 · Golden Hour
*City Portrait · PDF page 4*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-01-selected.png|850]]

A sunlit street portrait with honey blonde waves, a beige coat, layered necklaces, and bicycles receding into a city backdrop. The image centers an uncompromising amber cast and a close, eye-level selfie perspective.

**Art direction**
Late-afternoon warmth, natural facial expression, soft background, shallow depth of field. Keep the urban setting understated.
