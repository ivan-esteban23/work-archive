### 35 · Navy Eyelet
*A runway beauty close-up: dark brown wet-look hair · PDF page 28*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-35-selected.png|850]]

slicked back with a side part and loose face-framing strands, light blue-gray eyes with a graphic winged liner, peach-nude lips and a structured navy high-neck knit with bronze eyelets and raised stitched line panels. Blurred models and legs walk in the white background.

**Art direction**
Sleek wet-look hair, sharp liner, deep navy against a bright white runway, futuristic tailoring.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic vertical runway beauty close-up of an original adult woman with dark brown hair in a wet-look, side-parted slick-back style with a few loose strands falling across her face, light blue-gray eyes with a sharp black winged eyeliner, defined brows and glossy peach-nude lips. She wears a structured deep navy high-neck knit top with raised stitched geometric line panels across the chest and shoulders and bronze metal eyelets. Bright white fashion-show set, softly blurred models and legs walking in the background, crisp even runway lighting, shallow depth of field, sharp focus on the eyes, deep navy against white palette, high-fashion catwalk photography, no text or logos.
