### 53 · Ivory Bloom
*Full-length look against a pale gray studio wall: long dark · PDF page 40*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-53-selected.png|850]]

brown hair, a cropped ivory mohair cardigan with tiny buttons, a matching knit mini skirt layered over a sheer fluffy maxi skirt, all dotted with handmade 3D flowers, plus white square-toe flats and a pearl-beaded bag holding peach gerberas.

**Art direction**
Tonal ivory, sheer texture, floral appliqué, soft light and a stack of vintage papers on the floor.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Full-length vertical fashion editorial of an original adult East Asian woman with long, wavy dark brown hair, soft rosy cheeks and a calm, direct gaze, standing in a relaxed pose and holding a pearl-beaded handbag filled with peach and yellow gerbera daisies. She wears a cropped ivory mohair cardigan with small round buttons and handmade three-dimensional knit flowers, a matching ivory knit mini skirt layered over a sheer, airy mohair maxi skirt with flower appliqués, and white square-toe ballet flats. Pale warm-gray studio wall, soft gray floor with a diagonal window-light pattern, a small stack of vintage papers in the corner, tonal cream palette, no text or logos.

### 54 · Lilac Rosette
*Full-length look on a seamless light gray backdrop: long · PDF page 41*

Soft lilac against black hardware, sweet-meets-edgy contrast, clean studio minimalism.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Full-length vertical studio fashion photograph of an original adult East Asian woman with long, straight black hair framing her face, a soft natural expression and fresh pink lips, standing with her arms relaxed at her sides. She wears a fitted lilac ribbed long-sleeve mini dress with a keyhole neckline tied with thin ribbons and small fabric rosettes, a ruffled wave-shaped flower trim along the hem, sheer white slouch socks and chunky black leather foldover boots with silver hardware. Seamless pale gray-lavender backdrop, soft even studio light, muted pastel palette with black contrast, no text or logos.

### 56 · Winter Square
*A snowy selfie in a historic city square: long straight · PDF page 42*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-56-selected.png|850]]

blonde hair, blue eyes and windburnt pink cheeks under a black fur ushanka hat, with a black wool coat and black leather gloves. A colorful onion-domed cathedral and a red-brick tower rise behind her under an overcast sky.

**Art direction**
Cold-weather glamour, black fur against pale snow, rosy cheeks, landmark backdrop.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic vertical smartphone-style travel selfie of an original adult woman with long, straight blonde hair, blue eyes, rosy wind-flushed cheeks and soft pink lips, looking into the lens with a calm expression and one black leather-gloved hand held at her chest. She wears a black faux-fur ushanka hat with ear flaps and a black wool coat over a black turtleneck. Behind her, a wide snow-covered historic square with a colorful onion-domed cathedral on the left and a red-brick fortress wall with a tall clock tower on the right, under a heavy gray winter sky with light snowflakes. Natural cold daylight, black, white and brick-red palette, no text or logos.

### 58 · Beret & Pearls
*A polished beauty portrait against a light gray gradient: a · PDF page 43*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-58-selected.png|850]]

black wool beret with a gold medallion, black hair in a low braided bun with loose face-framing strands, winged liner, glossy rose lips, layered pearl and gold-chain necklaces and a black square-neck top.

**Art direction**
French-chic black with pearl and gold layering, dewy skin, soft studio gradient.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic vertical beauty portrait of an original adult East Asian woman with black hair in a low braided bun and loose wispy strands framing her face, a black wool beret with a small round gold medallion, a beauty mark on her cheek, sharp black winged eyeliner, peach-pink blush and glossy rose lips slightly parted, head tilted with her gaze on the camera. She wears layered necklaces of pearl strands, a gold chain with pearl drops and a long gold pendant chain over a black square-neck top. Soft light-gray studio gradient background, dewy luminous skin, black, pearl and gold palette, no text, watermark or logos.
