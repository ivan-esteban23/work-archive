### 15 · Amber Blouse
*Long, loose chestnut waves, a rust-orange floral print · PDF page 15*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-15-selected.png|850]]

blouse, layered silver necklaces and corduroy brown trousers. Seated pose with an upward, thoughtful gaze against a soft gray-green concrete wall.

**Art direction**
1970s-inspired bohemian portrait, warm rust and chestnut tones, natural skin texture, dreamy and introspective.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic vertical editorial portrait of an original adult woman with very long, loose chestnut-brown waves parted in the center, light blue eyes, soft freckles and natural skin texture. She sits leaning slightly forward with her hands resting near her knees, gazing up and away from the camera with a quiet, thoughtful expression. She wears a sheer rust-orange floral-print blouse with a pointed collar and ruffled cuffs, two delicate silver chain necklaces with a small round pendant, and dark brown corduroy trousers. Soft overcast daylight, muted gray-green blurred concrete wall background, warm rust and brown palette, 1970s bohemian mood, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, no text or logos.
