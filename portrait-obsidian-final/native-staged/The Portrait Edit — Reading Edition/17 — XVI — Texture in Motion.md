### 72 · Sage Tweed
*Runway Ribbon · PDF page 52*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-72-selected.png|850]]

A runway look in a pale sage tweed mini dress with patch pockets and a sweetheart neckline, worn over a white shirt with a voluminous white satin bow at the collar. White knee socks, pointed white slingback heels and a single pale ranunculus stem complete the look.

**Art direction**
Soft sage and cream against a warm amber stage glow, clean runway framing and a poised, neutral walk.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic full-length vertical runway photograph of an original adult woman with long, wavy copper-red hair framing her face, a poised neutral expression and fresh pale lips, walking toward the camera. She wears a fitted pale sage tweed mini dress with a sweetheart neckline, patch pockets and small pearl buttons, layered over a white shirt with a voluminous white satin bow at the collar and sheer white cape sleeves. White knee-high socks, pointed white slingback heels, and a single pale peach ranunculus held in one hand. Warm amber stage backdrop, soft spotlight from above, slightly textured concrete runway floor, pastel sage and cream palette, crisp tweed texture, contemporary runway editorial, no text or logos.

### 73 · Lace Back
*Street Utility · PDF page 53*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-73-selected.png|850]]

A low, crouched studio pose in a black sleeveless top with a corset-style lace-up back, a sage-green utility mini skirt with a wide belt and tall black platform boots. Long dark hair falls over one shoulder against a cool blue-grey backdrop.

**Art direction**
Blue-grey studio backdrop, black and olive utility tones, a low, relaxed pose with strong contour and a soft key light.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic vertical studio fashion portrait of an original adult woman in a crouched, relaxed pose, long glossy dark hair falling forward over one shoulder, a soft gaze toward the camera and natural makeup. She wears a fitted black sleeveless top with a corset-style lace-up back, an olive-green utility mini skirt with a wide D-ring belt that trails down, and tall black leather platform knee boots with chunky lug soles. Smooth blue-grey seamless studio backdrop, soft directional key light creating gentle contour on the arms and legs, muted black and olive palette, clean urban editorial styling, no text or logos.

### 74 · Velvet Rose
*Event Velvet · PDF page 53*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-74-selected.png|850]]

A short black velvet dress with puffed, fitted sleeves, a sheer illusion neckline trimmed in black, and flared cuffs. Hair pinned into a high bun, with a single pale pink rose held low in front of the body against a soft pink backdrop.

**Art direction**
Black velvet against a soft blush backdrop, a poised, direct-to-lens pose and a single bloom as the only accent.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic vertical three-quarter studio portrait of an original adult woman with dark brown hair pinned into a high, smooth bun with a few soft face-framing strands, defined brows, winged eyeliner and a calm, direct gaze with soft rosy lips. She wears a short black velvet dress with fitted puffed shoulders, long sleeves with flared cuffs, and a sheer black-trimmed illusion neckline. She holds a single pale pink rose low in front of her, hands loosely crossed. Soft blush-pink seamless backdrop with a deep burgundy fabric fold at the edge of frame, even diffused studio light, black and blush palette, velvet texture, polished red-carpet editorial, no text, watermark or logos.

### 75 · Caramel Coat
*Tawny Shag · PDF page 54*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-75-selected.png|850]]

A long, caramel-brown shaggy knit coat with an oversized shawl collar worn open over a suede-look brown mini dress with a gold-sequin trim at the neckline and hem. Cream ribbed socks and black lace-up boots with buckles complete the look against a warm wood-panel wall.

**Art direction**
Tonal caramel and tawny browns, a warm wood-panel backdrop, rich fuzzy and suede textures and an even, cool floor.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic full-length vertical fashion portrait of an original adult woman with very long, straight dark hair blowing gently across her face, a calm, direct gaze and a composed expression. She wears a long caramel-brown shaggy knit coat with an oversized shawl collar and ribbed cuffs, worn open over a fitted brown suede-look mini dress with a gold sequin trim at the neckline and hem and small gold buttons. Cream ribbed socks, tall black lace-up leather boots with buckle straps. Warm honey-toned wood-panel wall, soft even studio light, cool grey-green floor, monochrome caramel and brown palette, tactile fur and suede texture, autumn fashion editorial, no text or logos.

### 76 · Smocked Denim
*Blue on Blue · PDF page 54*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-76-selected.png|850]]

A crouched, low pose in an off-the-shoulder smocked top with ruffled edges and a matching denim-look mini skirt, paired with black mesh Mary Jane flats. Long dark hair falls past the shoulders, with one hand resting near the chin against a deep blue backdrop.

**Art direction**
Tonal blues from the denim to the backdrop, a soft, slightly angled pose and clean, even studio light.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic vertical studio fashion portrait of an original adult woman in a low, crouched pose with one hand resting near her chin, long dark wavy hair falling over her shoulders, soft blush, a calm, slightly pensive gaze and soft rosy lips. She wears an off-the-shoulder smocked top in washed mid-blue denim-look fabric with ruffled edges and thin straps, a matching denim-look mini skirt with a soft wash, and black mesh Mary Jane flats. Seamless deep blue studio backdrop, soft even light with gentle shadows, tonal blue palette, crisp fabric texture, clean summer editorial, no text or logos.

### 77 · Noir Tweed
*Schoolbook Noir · PDF page 55*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-77-selected.png|850]]

A full-length studio look in an oversized black tweed jacket with a white collar, pearl buttons and wide patch pockets, over black shorts and tall black buckled boots. An open old book rests on the floor beside her, casting a long shadow on a white wall.

**Art direction**
Black and white against a bright studio wall, strong shadow play, crisp tweed texture and a stiff, poised stance.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic full-length vertical studio fashion photograph of an original adult woman with dark hair pulled back loosely, a neutral, serious expression and natural makeup, standing straight and facing the camera with her arms relaxed at her sides. She wears an oversized black textured tweed jacket with a crisp white collar, large pearl buttons and wide patch pockets, over a fitted black pleated mini skirt and short black shorts, white ribbed knee socks and tall black leather boots with multiple silver buckle straps. An open old book with cream pages rests on the floor beside her, casting a long soft shadow across a bright white plaster wall. Soft diffused daylight, black and white palette, crisp tweed texture, clean minimalist fashion editorial, no text or logos.
