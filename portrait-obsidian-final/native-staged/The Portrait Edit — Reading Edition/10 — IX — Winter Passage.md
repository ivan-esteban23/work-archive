### 29 · Clean Slate
*A frontal, passport-style beauty study: dark hair slicked · PDF page 24*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-29-selected.png|850]]

back with a center part and two small top knots, bare skin with light freckles, pale gray-blue eyes, strong straight brows and a white ribbed short-sleeve top against a pale gray wall.

**Art direction**
Raw casting-photo minimalism, no retouching feel, soft flat daylight, neutral palette.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic casting-style portrait of an original adult woman with dark hair slicked back damp with a center part and two small tied knots at the top, light freckles, pale gray-blue eyes, strong natural brows, bare skin with no makeup and neutral pink lips. She faces the camera straight on with a calm, serious expression, shoulders square, wearing a white ribbed fitted short-sleeve top. Plain pale gray wall, soft even daylight, true-to-life skin texture with natural pores, minimal retouching, sharp focus on the eyes, no text or logos.

### 30 · Indigo Ridge
*A standing portrait in front of a mossy stone wall: long · PDF page 25*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-30-selected.png|850]]

dark hair with wispy bangs, rosy cheeks, a navy indigo-dyed wrap jacket with a red-and-white embroidered collar, a cream waist sash with blue cords, a pleated dark skirt with bright blue and orange woven hem bands and a velvet choker.

**Art direction**
Folk-inspired mountain heritage, deep indigo against warm stone, soft overcast travel-editorial light.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic vertical three-quarter travel editorial of an original adult East Asian woman with long, straight dark hair and soft wispy bangs, rosy blush on her cheeks and a calm, composed gaze. She wears a handmade navy indigo-dyed wrap jacket with a red and white embroidered collar, a cream fabric waist sash tied with blue cords, a finely pleated dark skirt with bright blue and orange woven hem bands, a dark velvet choker and small drop earrings. She stands in front of an old rounded-stone wall covered in moss and leaves in a mountain village. Soft overcast natural light, shallow depth of field, rich indigo blue against warm earthy stone tones, medium-format film look, authentic cultural-heritage fashion mood, no text or logos.

### 34 · Crossing Blue
*An urban street portrait at a busy neon-lit crossing: · PDF page 27*

![[Attachments/The Portrait Edit - Issue 01/study-34-selected.png|850]]

shoulder-length dark hair blown across the face, a navy oversized tailored blazer over a black tee, natural skin and a calm direct gaze. The city blurs into high-rise glass and colorful billboards.

**Art direction**
Metropolitan editorial, bright midday sun, crisp natural makeup, saturated city bokeh.

> [!example]- Image prompt
> Photorealistic vertical street-style editorial portrait of an original adult East Asian woman with shoulder-length dark brown hair, a few strands blown across her face by the wind, natural makeup with soft rosy lips and a calm, direct gaze. She wears an oversized navy tailored blazer over a plain black crew-neck top. Standing at a busy modern city crossing in bright midday sun, the background softly blurred into glass skyscrapers, colorful abstract billboards with no readable text, and green trees. Natural skin texture, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens, warm sunlight with cool navy tones, contemporary fashion magazine style, no text, watermark or logos.
