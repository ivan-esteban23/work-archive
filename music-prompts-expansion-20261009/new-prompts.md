
## More focused listening journeys

Added **2026-10-09**. Use these with the shared quality add-on above. They request playlist plans; append the optional save request when you want a new playlist saved. The suggested names describe listening themes, not claims about the music's reputation.

### 23. Death Metal — Riff Anatomy

```text
Spotify, curate a 16-track “Death Metal — Riff Anatomy” listening journey in four chapters: groove and weight, tremolo-driven momentum, rhythmic complexity, and melodic phrasing. Use four contrasting tracks per chapter and no more than two per artist. Keep death metal central and label hybrids. Explain what to listen for in each track without inventing timestamps or claiming you listened to audio you cannot access. End with three questions that will help me identify which riff approaches I enjoy. Verify identities, original years, and catalog matches; use fewer tracks if necessary.
```
