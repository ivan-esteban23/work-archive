## Research lanes
### Los Angeles
- Deathrock, early club and independent-label context.
- Leads: 45 Grave, Christian Death, Kommunity FK, Super Heroines and Voodoo Church.
- Look for period flyers, interviews, original catalog numbers and primary-source images.
### United Kingdom
- Batcave, theatrical post-punk, independent singles and fanzines.
- Leads: Specimen, Sex Gang Children, Ausgang and Alien Sex Fiend.
### Continental Europe
- Compare German post-punk and later revival networks without equating every coldwave act with deathrock.
- Leads: Xmal Deutschland, modern Leipzig deathrock and the existing label directory.
