## China

Heritage details · utility · playful color · dark streetwear

![Four AI-generated China outfit examples](images/china-lookbook.png)

**AI-generated examples. Read left to right.**

### Xin Zhongshi

A stand-collar blouse with a mamianqun: look for the broad flat front panel and pleated sides. Jade and ivory keep this example quiet and wearable.

*Context: Contemporary heritage fusion; the generated skirt is an interpretation of mamianqun construction.*

### Safaricore

An olive hiking jacket, cargo skirt, bucket hat, and practical sneakers. Pockets and outdoor fabrics are the main visual cues.

*Context: Urban utility styling, rather than an expedition outfit.*

### Kidcore

A bright color-block knit, relaxed denim, playful hair clips, and a bag charm. The recognizable ingredient is joyful color.

*Context: Shown on an adult; the name describes the aesthetic.*

### Opiumcore

Black leather, loose dark trousers, silver hardware, and chunky boots. Compare the darker mood with the other three looks.

*Context: One gothic streetwear interpretation; the report’s popularity figures remain unverified.*

## South Korea

Relaxed tailoring · neutral layering · casual hanbok fusion · sport mix

![Four AI-generated South Korea outfit examples](images/korea-lookbook.png)

**AI-generated examples. Read left to right.**

### K-Minimal

An oversized trench, simple knit, and wide tailored trousers. Calm neutrals and a clean silhouette do most of the work.

*Context: An editorial category used in the supplied report, not a universal description of Korean fashion.*

### Acubi

A cropped bolero, asymmetric layered top, washed-gray wide jeans, and a compact nylon bag. Focus on the layers and proportions.

*Context: A neutral Y2K-inspired interpretation; the supplied report’s timing claims were not independently audited.*

### Hanbok fusion

A mint jeogori-inspired jacket with front ties, straight denim, sneakers, and a small tassel ornament. One heritage piece anchors a practical outfit.

*Context: Casual fusion, separate from ceremonial hanbok. Compare structure with designer photography before publication.*

### Jersey chic

An oversized football jersey with a pleated skirt and technical sneakers. The sports top becomes everyday fashion through styling.

*Context: Sport mixing appears across markets; this Seoul setting does not make it uniquely Korean.*

## Japan

Menswear proportions · romantic grunge · ballet textures · seasonal dress

![Four AI-generated Japan outfit examples](images/japan-lookbook.png)

**AI-generated examples. Read left to right.**

### MEN’SLIKE

A roomy blazer and shirt, a tie, barrel-leg jeans, loafers, and a plush bag charm. Compare the structured top with the curved denim volume.

*Context: An androgynous styling option within a much broader Japanese fashion landscape.*

### Fairy grunge

Lace and a soft dress under darker outerwear, finished with chunky boots. The contrast between romantic texture and heavy footwear explains the look.

*Context: An illustrative Harajuku hybrid; the linked Tokyo article does not establish this specific micro-style.*

### Balletcore

A pink cardigan, tulle midi skirt, leg warmers, and ballet flats, paired with a sporty bag. The dance-inspired details are adapted for streetwear.

*Context: An illustrative styling idea from the report; not a claim that every pictured item is currently trending.*

### Summer yukata

An indigo cotton robe, obi, geta, and a small drawstring bag. Lanterns place the look in a summer festival context.

*Context: Seasonal traditional dress. Check wrap direction and garment construction before using a generated image as a reference.*
